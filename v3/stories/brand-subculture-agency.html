Subculture Agency is a Los Angeles-based artist booking and management company specializing in underground electronic music. Representing artists across house, techno, bass music, and experimental electronic genres, Subculture connects talent with the right opportunities without compromising artistic integrity.

The agency has relationships with underground venues, festivals, and promoters throughout California and beyond, ensuring that artists on their roster get access to opportunities that align with their artistic vision — not just the biggest paycheck.

Artist booking, artist management, tour coordination, brand partnerships, and career development for underground electronic music artists based in and around Los Angeles.
