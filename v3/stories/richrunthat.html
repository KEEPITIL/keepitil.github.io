Anaheim is Disneyland's city. But behind the theme park tourism and the Angels Stadium crowd, there's a real underground electronic music scene operating in the city's back channels, house venues, and off-the-map warehouse spaces — and Richrunthat is one of its architects. Based in OC's largest city, Richrunthat has been building a reputation through consistent energy, genuine community focus, and sets that remind Anaheim's underground that the real magic happens after midnight and well off the main strip. This isn't music for visitors. It's music for locals who know the city better than any tourist map ever could, and who find their connection to each other in the shared space of a well-crafted underground set. Richrunthat represents a strain of Orange County underground culture that refuses to apologize for existing outside of the spotlight — and in that refusal, they've built something that matters.
