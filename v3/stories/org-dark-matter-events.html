Dark Matter Events is a Los Angeles event organization dedicated exclusively to underground techno — no crossover programming, no genre compromise, just focused, serious techno events that serve the specific community of LA listeners who want the real thing rather than a commercial approximation of it.

Founded by longtime LA techno heads who felt that the commercial techno market — Factory 93's festival-scale events, the major club techno circuit — wasn't serving the underground community's need for smaller, more intimate, more musically demanding experiences, Dark Matter Events fills that gap with rigorous programming and uncompromising sound quality.

Factory 93 does what they do extremely well at their scale. But there's a community of LA techno listeners who want something different: smaller rooms, longer sets, more demanding music, prices that don't require a significant financial commitment to attend. We're not competing with Factory 93; we're serving a different audience that they're not trying to serve.

Networking, listening, and attending events in other markets. The international underground techno circuit is relatively small and well-connected. Artists who are doing the right thing for the right reasons tend to find each other regardless of geography, and KEEPITIL's network has been useful for making some of those connections more visible.
