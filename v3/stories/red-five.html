The Disco Tech Lane

Red Five describes the style as “disco tech” — not to be confused with tech-house, but rather the space where late-70s disco production aesthetics meet modern electronic arrangement. Vocoder runs, hand-clap patterns, four-on-the-floor kicks with melodic basslines.

OC Roots

Red Five has been playing OC venues since 2021, building a loyal following through residencies and underground collectives. The booking profile has expanded steadily into LA and SD, with a sound that translates across both the intimate club setting and the outdoor festival stage.
