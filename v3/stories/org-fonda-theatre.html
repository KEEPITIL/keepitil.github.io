The Fonda Theatre is a historic 1,300-capacity music venue in Hollywood, CA, operated by Goldenvoice. Originally opened in 1926 as a cinema, the Fonda is now one of Hollywood's most beloved mid-size concert venues, known for its intimate atmosphere and excellent acoustics.
