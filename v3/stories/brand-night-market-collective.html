Night Market Collective is a Los Angeles-based curatorial collective that organizes rotating vendor markets at underground electronic music events across SoCal — bringing together independent makers, artists, food vendors, and creatives in a curated market format that fits the aesthetic and values of the events they serve.

Unlike conventional event vendor setups, Night Market Collective operates as a curatorial exercise — each market is handpicked for quality, aesthetic coherence, and community alignment. Vendors are primarily local independent makers whose work connects to underground music culture in some meaningful way.

We look for genuine community connection, product quality, and aesthetic alignment with the event. A vendor who's been attending underground events for years and started making things for the community is a very different thing from a vendor who heard there's money at EDM events. We want the former.

The collective structure means we share resources, knowledge, and connections. Vendors help each other set up, refer customers between booths, and collaborate on event opportunities. It's built on the same values as the underground events we serve — community over competition.
