Goldenvoice is a Los Angeles-based concert and festival promotion company, best known as the producer of the Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival. Founded in 1981 by Gary Tovar, Goldenvoice has been at the center of LA's live music scene for over four decades.

Now a subsidiary of AEG Presents, Goldenvoice produces hundreds of concerts annually at venues across LA and Southern California, from club shows at the Palladium to major festivals like Stagecoach and Splash House.

Concert promotion, festival production (Coachella, Stagecoach, Desert Trip), venue management, and artist bookings throughout Southern California.

Goldenvoice events include Coachella (April), Stagecoach (April), FYF Fest, and year-round concerts at the Hollywood Palladium and other LA venues. Visit axs.com for tickets.
