True House LA is a Southern California house music collective, record label, and event promoter founded in 2013 by longtime DJ and producer Joel DeMarzo. The project was built on a simple idea — to spread the feeling of house music and give producers an organic way to release their work — and it has grown into one of the region's steadiest underground house communities across both Los Angeles and Orange County.

As a promoter, True House LA throws regular events spanning classic house, deep house, tech house, and the afro house sound that has surged through SoCal floors in recent years. Their nights move between LA rooms and Orange County venues like The Circle OC in Huntington Beach, building a crowd that shows up for the music rather than the headliner name on the flyer.

Hard techno and bass tend to grab the headlines, but house is the backbone of California's dance tradition — and True House LA has spent more than a decade keeping that backbone strong. By spanning both LA and Orange County and pairing a record label with a live events brand, the collective gives house producers and DJs a real platform on both sides of the county line.

KEEPITIL features True House LA as part of mapping the organizers shaping Southern California's underground electronic music landscape.
