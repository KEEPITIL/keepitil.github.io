KEEPITIL is a Los Angeles-based underground music and culture platform dedicated to spotlighting the artists, organizers, and brands who move the underground scene forward. Founded with the mission to document and amplify the raw energy of California's rave, bass, and house communities, KEEPITIL is more than a media outlet — it's a movement.

From OC warehouse parties to SF techno lofts, KEEPITIL covers events, DJs, and culture across the entire state. The platform features a DJ roster, event listings, organizer directory, and editorial content that gives underground artists a stage they deserve.

KEEPITIL covers underground events from OC, LA, San Diego, the Bay Area, and the Inland Empire. The platform curates artist spotlights, event recaps, and cultural commentary. DJs and organizers can apply to be featured on the roster and directory.

KEEPITIL regularly covers local underground events, warehouse raves, and after-parties. Follow @keepitil on Instagram for real-time event coverage and announcements.
