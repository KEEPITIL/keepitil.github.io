Resonance Events is a San Diego-based underground event organization focused specifically on the deeper, more demanding end of the electronic music spectrum — events programmed for listeners who want to go somewhere, not just dance somewhere. Deep techno, minimal, experimental electronics, and the intersection of bass and ambient music are their programming territory.

The collective takes its name seriously: resonance is what happens when the music and the room and the audience align in a way that produces something greater than the parts. Resonance Events events are designed to create that alignment through careful venue selection, acoustic attention, and programming that trusts the audience to follow the music wherever it goes.

With collaborative relationships with specific Tijuana collectives who share our programming values. We co-produce events on both sides of the border, bring TJ artists to SD audiences and SD artists to TJ venues, and generally work to make the border feel like a connection rather than a division. The electronic music cultures on both sides have been influencing each other for decades; we're just making that explicit.

Through extensive exploration and relationship building with venue operators who understand that the acoustic environment matters. Many event organizers treat the venue as a backdrop. For us, the venue is part of the artistic statement — the sound of the room is as important as the sound system in it.
