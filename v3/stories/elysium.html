The name says it all. elysium — the place of perfect happiness in Greek mythology — is a Los Angeles Bass and Dubstep artist who believes heavy music can be transcendent music. Where others see bass weight as aggression, elysium finds the emotional core of it: the frequencies that don't just move bodies but bypass the mind entirely and speak directly to something deeper. LA's underground has been listening, and what they've heard is a DJ who treats sub-bass as a spiritual medium rather than a blunt instrument. In a city that has seen every possible version of electronic music come and go, elysium has found the version that feels genuinely new.
