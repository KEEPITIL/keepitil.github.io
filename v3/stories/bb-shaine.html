Some DJs pick a lane and defend it. BB Shaine treats the borders between genres as the place where the real energy lives. The Los Angeles producer and DJ is recognized for high-energy performances built on technical four-deck mixing, moving without warning between the bass weight of techno, the snap of electro and footwork, the swing of UKG, and the velocity of jungle and drum & bass. Tempos twist mid-set, but the pressure never lets up. Off the decks she is just as active in the scene itself — curating events, championing emerging artists, and releasing music through the GRN+GLD collective and label she helps drive.
