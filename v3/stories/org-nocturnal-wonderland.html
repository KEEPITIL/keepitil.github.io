Nocturnal Wonderland is one of the longest-running rave events in the United States, produced by Insomniac Events since 1995. Held annually at the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino, Nocturnal is a Labor Day weekend tradition for California's rave community.

What began as intimate warehouse gatherings has evolved into a massive two-day festival with multiple stages, art installations, and tens of thousands of attendees — while still honoring its underground roots through artist selections and rave culture aesthetics.

Annual Labor Day weekend rave festival at NOS Events Center, featuring electronic music across multiple stages with full production, art, and camping.

Nocturnal Wonderland takes place every Labor Day weekend in San Bernardino, CA. Visit insomniac.com/festivals/nocturnal-wonderland for tickets and lineup.
