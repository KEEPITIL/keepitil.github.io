Underground OC is Costa Mesa's dedicated house music underground, curating deep, soulful, and dark house experiences for OC's most discerning listeners. With a philosophy rooted in musical quality over spectacle, Underground OC keeps things intimate and authentic.

The organization has built a reputation for impeccable sound and curation, bringing international house selectors to small OC venues where the connection between artist and crowd is electric.

Underground house music events, intimate venue experiences, international artist bookings, and deep house education for OC music fans.

Underground OC hosts monthly events in Costa Mesa and surrounding OC. Follow @undergroundoc for upcoming events.
