Whet Foundation is a San Diego-based electronic music organization dedicated to nurturing the city's underground electronic community. Known for their intimate events and commitment to quality over quantity, Whet has been a cornerstone of San Diego's independent music scene.

The organization produces events that blend electronic with indie and experimental sounds, creating a unique San Diego flavor that distinguishes the city's underground from the larger LA and SF scenes.
