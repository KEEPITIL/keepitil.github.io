San Diego has always been an underrated bass music city, and XEODIN is part of why. Operating at the intersection of Dubstep, Tearout, and Riddim — three genres that demand physical commitment from both artist and crowd — XEODIN builds sets that move bodies with the precision of someone who understands exactly where the low end needs to hit. The bass isn't just an element of the music; it's the architecture that everything else hangs from. San Diego's underground has found in XEODIN a DJ who treats the science of the drop with the seriousness it deserves and the instinct of an artist who was born into this sound.
