Signal Supply Co is an Irvine-based retailer and curator of professional audio accessories for DJs, producers, and live electronic artists in Southern California. Where most gear retailers are generalists, Signal Supply Co is a specialist — everything in the catalog is selected for performance in live DJ and production environments.

The Signal Supply catalog covers the accessories that make professional DJ setups function reliably: high-quality cables, phono cartridges, slipmats, protective cases, and the dozens of small components that the difference between a smooth set and a technical disaster. Staff are working DJs who've learned what matters through direct experience.

Because the accessories are where professional DJs actually face problems. The CDJ either works or it doesn't. But the cables, the connections, the cartridges, the storage — those are where sets get made or broken. We're filling that specific gap.

We go to events. We talk to artists at shows. We pay attention to what's causing problems in professional DJ setups and we make sure we stock the solutions. The catalog is a direct reflection of what working DJs have told us they need.
