Westminster sits at the heart of Orange County, and Smoke operates at the heart of Westminster's underground music scene. A DJ who treats genre like a suggestion rather than a rule, Smoke moves between House's warmth, Minimal's precision, Techno's force, Afro House's percussion, and Latin House's groove with the ease of someone who learned music as a language before they learned it as a label. The result is sets that resist easy categorization — they move through moods and frequencies the way the best music always has, guided by feel rather than formula. In a region where DJs often pick a lane and stay in it, Smoke has built something rarer: a sound that is entirely its own.
