Auntie. is a community at its core — a Los Angeles-based collective that champions marginalized voices and puts joy, visibility, and unfiltered expression front and center. Founded by DJs Etari and Lechuga, it has become a staple of LA's underground warehouse scene, celebrating femme and queer talent on dancefloors built for everyone.

An Auntie. party is a fast-paced, genre-fluid delight, moving between deep foot-to-the-floor basslines, techno, and house across a single night. Events land at rotating DTLA venues, typically run 21+ with tickets in the $15–35 range, and start late — the kind of intimate, style-forward room where the lineup matters more than the headliner's clout.

LA's rave underground is at its strongest when it makes space for everyone, and Auntie. has built exactly that — intimate shows with diverse lineups, on-point style, and a clear commitment to femme and queer artists. Recent bills have featured names like Kim Ann Foxman, Mor Elian, Doctor Jeep, and KEEPITIL roster artist John Bryars alongside founders Etari and Lechuga, tying the party directly into the city's wider underground network.

KEEPITIL features Auntie. as part of mapping the organizers shaping Southern California's underground electronic music landscape.
