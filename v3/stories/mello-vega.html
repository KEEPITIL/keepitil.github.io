The Latin House Connection

Latin house as a genre has always been underrepresented in the SoCal underground despite the obvious cultural connection. Mello Vega brings that sound with authenticity — not as a novelty, but as a genuine contribution to what the scene sounds like here.

Live Energy

A Mello Vega set moves. The energy curves are sharp, the builds are playful, and the drops hit with a percussive physicality that gets crowds moving in ways straight house sometimes doesn’t. It’s a unique thing to watch.
