Bass Head Apparel is a Los Angeles-based streetwear brand built for the bass music community. Blending underground rave aesthetics with LA streetwear culture, Bass Head creates clothing that moves between the warehouse and the street without missing a beat.

The brand has collaborated with underground artists and DJs throughout California, releasing limited-edition collections that capture the energy of bass culture in wearable form. Every piece is designed for the community, by the community.
