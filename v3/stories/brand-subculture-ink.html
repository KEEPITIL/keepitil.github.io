Subculture Ink is a Huntington Beach tattoo studio and custom art space with deep roots in the Orange County underground electronic music community. Founded by artists who are also longtime underground event attendees, the studio's aesthetic draws directly from rave and electronic music visual culture — geometric patterns, dark palettes, and the specific visual language of underground event art.

Subculture Ink operates at the intersection of fine art, tattoo craft, and underground music culture. The studio hosts occasional music events, collaborates with local artists on prints and apparel, and maintains an active presence at underground events across OC. It's as much a community hub as a business.

The clients who find us are often from the scene, and the work they commission reflects that — geometric patterns, darker palettes, imagery that references the visual culture of underground events. Our artists are in the scene themselves, which means they bring genuine context rather than just executing what a client describes.

A community rather than just a customer base. The underground scene creates people who are committed, values-driven, and interested in things that are built for love rather than profit. Those are exactly the kind of clients we want, and the kind of community we want to be part of.
