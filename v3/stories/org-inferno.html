Inferno OC/IE is a Southern California underground event organization based in Corona, serving both Orange County and the Inland Empire. With a multi-genre approach that spans bass music, house, and open-format underground sounds, Inferno bridges the gap between OC and IE's underground communities.

Located at the crossroads between OC and the IE, Inferno is uniquely positioned to draw from both regions' underground scenes, creating events that feel both local and connected to a broader SoCal community.
