Insomniac Events is one of the world's largest electronic music event companies, headquartered in Los Angeles. Founded in 1993, Insomniac is responsible for some of the biggest electronic music festivals in the world, including Electric Daisy Carnival (EDC) Las Vegas, Beyond Wonderland, and Nocturnal Wonderland.

What began as small warehouse parties in the early LA rave scene has grown into a global operation producing hundreds of events annually across North America, Europe, and beyond — while remaining committed to the culture and community that started it all.

Large-scale music festivals, nightclub events, brand partnerships, artist development, and electronic music culture preservation through their Insomniac Records label and content division.

Insomniac produces dozens of major events annually in California, including EDC SoCal, Beyond Wonderland SoCal, Nocturnal Wonderland, and ongoing nightclub residencies at venues across LA. Visit insomniac.com for full event calendar.
