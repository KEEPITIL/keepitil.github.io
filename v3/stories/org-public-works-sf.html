Public Works is one of San Francisco's most celebrated underground electronic music venues, operating a two-room nightclub in the Mission District. Known for its diverse programming, exceptional sound system, and commitment to underground culture, Public Works has been a cornerstone of SF's club scene since opening.

The venue's programming spans house, techno, disco, bass, and experimental electronic music, with both international headliners and strong support for local Bay Area artists. Public Works is known for fostering an inclusive, music-forward community.
