Underground Prints is a Los Angeles design and print studio specializing in the specific needs of underground event promoters — from flyer design rooted in rave visual culture to high-quality offset and digital printing and distribution networks that reach the record stores, coffee shops, and community spaces where underground audiences actually are.

The studio's founders are graphic designers with deep roots in the rave and underground music scene who got tired of watching promoters pay for generic design that didn't reflect the culture of their events. Underground Prints brings authentic visual knowledge to every project — they know the visual history of rave culture and apply it with intention.

It references a visual lineage — the UK rave flyers of the late 80s and early 90s, the specific typography conventions that developed in house and techno visual culture, the color theory of printing for dark environments. Generic event design treats an event like a product to be marketed. Rave design treats it like a cultural artifact to be documented.

Education through reference. We show them the visual history of rave culture and work backward from what resonates to understand their specific event's aesthetic identity. Most promoters have a clear sense of the music and community they're building; the visual identity just needs to catch up.
