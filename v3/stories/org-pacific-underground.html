Pacific Underground is a San Diego-based event collective with a specific mission: building cross-regional connections between the SD, OC, and LA underground scenes. Their events rotate between all three markets, bringing artists from each region to audiences in the others and building the regional relationships that sustain the broader SoCal underground.

The collective runs events at carefully selected venues in each of the three markets — venues chosen for sound quality, underground aesthetic, and community alignment rather than capacity or commercial cachet. Pacific Underground events are recognized throughout the SoCal corridor as reliably high-quality underground experiences.

Because the three scenes — SD, OC, and LA — are geographically close but culturally isolated from each other. Artists from SD don't automatically get to OC gigs, and vice versa. We're solving that problem by creating a circuit that treats SoCal as a unified underground scene with regional chapters rather than three separate and disconnected markets.

With a permanent team in each city plus a shared event infrastructure. Each city team handles local relationships — venues, artists, promotion. The shared infrastructure handles things like sound system rental networks, liability coverage, and the regional communication that keeps the circuit coherent.
