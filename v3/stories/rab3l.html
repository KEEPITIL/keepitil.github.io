RAB3L is a Torrance DJ working the seam between bass house, tech house, UK garage, and bassline — a South Bay selector built for the low end.

Operating out of the South Bay corner of Los Angeles County, RAB3L lives in the bass-forward end of the underground: the swing of UK garage, the weight of bassline and bass house, and the tech-house groove that keeps a floor moving without ever letting up. It's music made for rooms that feel it in the chest before they hear it.

RAB3L has logged appearances in the Avalon Hollywood ecosystem — one of the venues that, in LA's scene, tends to confirm a working DJ rather than make one. The lane is clear: keep the pressure on, keep the groove honest, and let the sub do the talking.
