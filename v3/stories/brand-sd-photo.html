SD Photo Collective is a San Diego-based photography collective dedicated to documenting the city's underground music and arts scene. A team of photographers with deep roots in SD's rave and electronic music community, the collective provides event coverage that captures the authentic energy of underground culture.

Their work has appeared in underground media outlets, artist press kits, and event promotions throughout San Diego and Southern California. The collective actively supports local artists and organizers by providing affordable coverage options.

Event photography coverage, artist portraits, event recap photos, social media content, and underground scene documentation for San Diego's music and arts community.
