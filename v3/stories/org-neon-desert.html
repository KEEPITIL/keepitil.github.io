Neon Desert is a Riverside-based event collective that runs underground electronic music events across the Inland Empire and in the desert-edge communities east of the LA basin — territory that most SoCal promoters ignore and that Neon Desert has made into a distinctive circuit with its own character and community.

The collective's aesthetic is informed by its geography: the industrial landscape of the IE and the open desert spaces to the east create a visual and sonic context that makes Neon Desert events feel different from anything happening in LA or OC. Events happen in warehouses, desert properties, and unusual industrial spaces that the IE geography makes available.
