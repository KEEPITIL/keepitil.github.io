LA Visuals is a Los Angeles-based photography and video production company specializing in underground event coverage, artist photography, and music video production. With a deep connection to LA's underground electronic music scene, LA Visuals captures the authentic energy of live events in a way that outside photographers can't match.

The team has shot events at Exchange LA, Academy 1720, Sound Nightclub, and dozens of underground warehouse events throughout LA. Their work appears in editorial features, artist profiles, and event recaps across underground music media.

Event photography, artist portrait photography, event recap videos, live streaming, social media content creation, and music video production for underground artists and events.
