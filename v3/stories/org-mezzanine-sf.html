Mezzanine was one of San Francisco's most iconic electronic music venues, located in SoMa (South of Market) and operating for over two decades. The club was renowned for its world-class sound system, beautiful industrial space, and programming that shaped SF's techno and house scene.

While the original Mezzanine location closed in 2019, the brand and its legacy continue to influence the SF electronic music scene. The venue hosted virtually every major DJ in the world and was a proving ground for San Francisco's underground scene.

Historically — nightclub events, techno and house programming, international DJ bookings, and underground cultural events in San Francisco's SoMa district.
