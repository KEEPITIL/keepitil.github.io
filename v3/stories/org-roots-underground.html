Roots Underground is an Inglewood-based event collective dedicated to celebrating and centering the Black origins of electronic music — house, techno, Detroit, Chicago, and the communities that created them — through events, education, and intentional community building in South LA.

The collective's programming explicitly connects contemporary underground electronic music to its roots in Black American and Black British communities. Events feature artists who understand and honor that lineage, educational elements that provide context, and a community orientation that makes space specifically for communities of color who aren't always centered in mainstream electronic music spaces.

Because house music was made in Black gay clubs in Chicago. Techno was made by three Black kids in suburban Detroit. Jungle and UK garage were made in Black British communities. Those origins are not always acknowledged in contemporary electronic music culture, and the communities that created this music are not always centered in spaces that are ostensibly celebrating it. We're correcting that.

By actively making space rather than passively hoping people show up. That means the visual communication of our events, the artists we book, the venues we choose, the pricing structure, the way events are run — every element is considered for whether it signals welcome or signals that the default attendee is assumed to be white and middle-class. When you remove those signals, different people show up.
