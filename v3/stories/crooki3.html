Lake Elsinore sits 60 miles from LA and 40 miles from San Diego — which means crooki3 learned early that you have to travel for the music, or bring the music to you. This versatile DJ built a sound across House, Tech House, Latin House, and Techno that can hold a room regardless of what that room expects. The genre blend reflects California's cultural mix — the Latin heritage that runs through the Inland Empire, the club culture influences that flow in from both major cities, and the independent creative spirit that defines what the Riverside underground is becoming.
