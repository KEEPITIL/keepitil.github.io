PYPRR doesn't play music — they deploy it. Based in Los Angeles, this Hard Techno and Hardcore artist operates in the upper register of BPMs where tracks stop being music and start becoming a physical experience. LA's underground rave rooms have felt it. The energy is industrial, unrelenting, and completely intentional — every build, every drop, every transition engineered to push the crowd to a place they didn't know they needed to go. This is not background music. This is a confrontation, and PYPRR is very good at it.
