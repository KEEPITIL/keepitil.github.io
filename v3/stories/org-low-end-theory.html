Low End Theory is a legendary weekly beat music event series that ran at The Airliner in Lincoln Heights, Los Angeles. Founded in 2006, Low End Theory was the epicenter of LA's beat scene, providing a platform for artists like Flying Lotus, Thundercat, Tokimonsta, and countless others who went on to international success.

The night became globally influential, with Low End Theory nights appearing in cities around the world inspired by the LA original. The event series transformed how underground electronic music was presented and perceived, blending hip-hop production aesthetics with live performance energy.

Beat music event production, underground electronic showcases, artist development, and cultural programming for LA's producer and beat music community.

Low End Theory has undergone various hiatuses and revivals. Follow @lowendtheorynight for updates on current programming.
