The Do Lab is a Los Angeles-based art and music production company known for creating transformative festival experiences that blur the line between concert and art installation. Founded in 2004, the Do Lab produces Lightning in a Bottle — one of California's most beloved transformational festivals — and is responsible for the iconic Do LaB stage at Coachella.

The Do Lab is a Los Angeles-based art and music production company known for creating transformative festival experiences that blur the line between concert and art installation. Founded in 2004, the Do Lab produces Lightning in a Bottle — one of California's most beloved transformational festivals — and is responsible for the iconic Do LaB stage at Coachella.

The Do Lab's approach combines cutting-edge art installations, conscious community programming, and eclectic music curation across electronic, world, and experimental genres. Their events are designed as complete immersive worlds.

Festival production (Lightning in a Bottle), stage production at Coachella, art installations, transformational events, and conscious community experiences throughout California.

Lightning in a Bottle takes place annually in late May in Southern California. The Do LaB stage returns to Coachella each year. Visit dolabexperience.com for details.
