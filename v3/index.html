Frequently Asked Questions

What is KEEPITIL?

KEEPITIL is a discovery platform for OC, LA and San Diego's EDM scene — events, artists, brands, organizers, music and culture in one place.

How do I find EDM events in OC and LA?

Use the events calendar above — pick a month, filter by genre (house, techno, trance, bass and more), and scroll day by day. Each flyer links straight to tickets.

How do I save events I'm interested in?

Tap any flyer to open its ticket page. KEEPITIL Picks are highlighted with a green ribbon so you can spot the must-see events at a glance.

How can I list my event on KEEPITIL?

Submit it through the Submit an Event page, or become a partner to feature your event to the SoCal community.

Which genres and cities does KEEPITIL cover?

House, techno, trance, bass, DnB, garage, hip-hop, disco and country — across Orange County, Los Angeles and San Diego.
