About Underground Sound Co

Underground Sound Co is a Santa Ana-based sound system and PA company specializing in underground events, warehouse raves, and electronic music productions. Understanding that great music needs great sound, Underground Sound Co provides professional audio systems matched to any venue size from intimate 200-person gatherings to 2,000+ capacity warehouse events.

The team has deep roots in OC's underground scene, making them a trusted partner for organizers who need sound engineers who understand rave culture as much as audio engineering.

What They Offer

Sound system rental, PA systems, subwoofer arrays, DJ booth setup, live sound engineering, and technical consultation for underground events in OC and SoCal.
