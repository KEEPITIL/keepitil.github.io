ABOUT THE FOUNDRY COLLECTIVE

The Foundry Collective is a Los Angeles event organization based in the Arts District that produces events at the intersection of contemporary visual art and underground electronic music — events that combine live visual art, installation, and the best underground DJs in a warehouse format that treats the space as a canvas.

Founded by a collaboration between a visual artist and an electronic music promoter, The Foundry Collective has developed a reputation for events that are both musically and aesthetically exceptional — where the visual experience is as carefully curated as the lineup, and the two are designed to work together rather than in parallel.

Q&A
How do you integrate visual art and electronic music without one overwhelming the other?

By treating them as equal partners from the planning stage rather than adding art to an event that's already been designed around music, or vice versa. The visual artists we work with are part of the event conception from the beginning — the sonic and visual programs are developed simultaneously.

What makes the Arts District specifically right for this kind of event?

The physical infrastructure — industrial buildings with high ceilings, raw surfaces, and the visual character of a working warehouse — is itself an aesthetic statement. And the Arts District community includes a lot of people who move between visual art and electronic music naturally. We didn't have to create that intersection; we just recognized it was already there.
