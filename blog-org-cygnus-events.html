About Cygnus Events

Cygnus Events is a San Diego underground bass music collective specializing in dubstep, riddim, and bass-heavy electronic events. Operating out of America's Finest City, Cygnus has built a dedicated San Diego bass community that rivals the larger LA scene in energy and passion.

The collective is known for bringing touring bass artists to San Diego while actively developing local talent, creating a pipeline for SD producers and DJs to reach wider audiences.

What They Do

Bass music event production, dubstep and riddim shows, local artist development, and underground community building in San Diego.
