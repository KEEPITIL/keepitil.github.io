★ KEEPITIL PRESENTS
🎵 Listen to Nova Halcyon

Nova Halcyon builds sets like buildings — foundation first, then floor after floor of rolling low end until the whole room is somewhere else. Raised on the citrus-packing warehouses of Santa Ana, they turned borrowed gear and empty industrial rooms into a signature sound the SoCal underground now travels for.

"A set isn't a playlist. It's a room full of strangers agreeing to go somewhere together — my job is just to lead."
THE STORY
When did you start and what got you into it?
I started spinning at 16 in a friend's garage in Santa Ana — cheap controller, borrowed monitors, way too much fog. The first time a room actually moved to something I built, I was hooked for life.
Who influenced your sound most?
Early on it was the raw Detroit and Berlin techno records my older cousin left behind. Later, the SoCal warehouse crews taught me that a set is a story, not a playlist.
THE CRAFT
How would you describe your sound?
Hypnotic, physical warehouse techno — rolling low end, long builds, and one moment near the end where the whole room drops into darkness before the release.
What can someone expect when they book you?
A journey. I don't do peak-time-the-whole-time. Expect a real arc — a slow burn, a climb, and a finish people remember on the drive home.
OFF THE DECKS
What are you working on right now?
A four-track warehouse EP and a live hardware set I can take on the road without a laptop. Slower, heavier, more human.
