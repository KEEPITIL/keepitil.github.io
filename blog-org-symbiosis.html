About Symbiosis Gathering

Symbiosis Gathering is a Bay Area-born transformational festival that blends electronic music, art, sustainability, and community. Founded in 2004, Symbiosis takes place at various lake and outdoor locations in Northern California and attracts a global audience of artists, musicians, and conscious community members.

The festival is known for its stunning lakeside settings, massive art installations, and programming that spans electronic music, live performances, yoga, and sustainability workshops. Symbiosis represents the best of California's transformational festival culture.

What They Do

Transformational festival production, electronic music and arts programming, sustainability initiatives, and community-focused outdoor events in Northern California.

Upcoming Events

Symbiosis Gathering takes place biennially (every other year) in late September. Visit symbiosisgathering.com for the next edition details.
