Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last Updated: June 18, 2026

Overview

KEEPITIL ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website located at keepitil.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit the Site, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Site.

Information We Collect

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit KEEPITIL, certain information is collected automatically by our hosting infrastructure and third-party services, including:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location (country/region level)
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring URLs (how you arrived at our site)
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • Date and time of your visit

Information You Provide

If you contact us via email or submit the artist/brand/organizer application form, we collect the information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, Instagram handle, and any other details you include in your message.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

KEEPITIL and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us and our partners recognize your browser, remember preferences, and deliver relevant content and advertising.

Types of Cookies Used

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic site functionality.
  • Analytics cookies: Used to understand how visitors interact with the Site (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Advertising cookies: Used by Google AdSense and other advertising networks to deliver personalized ads based on your interests and browsing history.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings or the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

Google AdSense and Advertising

KEEPITIL uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses the DoubleClick cookie to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website and other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of the use of the DoubleClick cookie for interest-based advertising by visiting Ads Settings. Alternatively, you may opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for interest-based advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites. For more information about how Google uses data when you use our partner's sites or apps, visit How Google uses data from sites or apps that use our services.

Third-Party Services

Our Site uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics — Website traffic analysis. Privacy Policy
  • Google AdSense — Advertising delivery. Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages — Website hosting. Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — Font delivery. Privacy Policy
  • Unsplash — Photography used on article pages. Privacy Policy

We do not control these third parties and their privacy practices are not governed by this policy.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site
  • Understand how visitors use the Site (analytics)
  • Serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • Respond to inquiries submitted via email or our application form
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain information you voluntarily submit (e.g., via email) only as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected or as required by law. Analytics and advertising data is retained per the policies of the respective third-party providers (typically 26 months for Google Analytics).

Your Privacy Rights

California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to: know what personal information we collect about you; request deletion of your personal information; opt out of the sale of personal information (note: we do not sell personal information); and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

European Residents (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data; object to or restrict processing of your data; and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below.

All Users

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Note that blocking certain cookies may affect site functionality.

Children's Privacy

KEEPITIL is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

External Links

Our Site may contain links to external websites (event ticketing pages, artist social profiles, brand websites, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
