ABOUT SOCAL RAVE CO

SoCal Rave Co was founded in Fullerton by longtime underground event attendees who couldn't find apparel that reflected the actual aesthetic of California's underground scene — not the neon festival mainstream, but the darker, more intentional visual language of warehouse raves and intimate club nights.

Every piece in the SoCal Rave Co catalog is designed for people who take the music seriously. Technical fabrics that work at 3AM in a warehouse, graphics that reference the scene's visual history, and sizing that fits bodies that actually dance — it's apparel by the underground, for the underground.

Q&A
What separates your brand from the mainstream festival apparel market?

The mainstream festival market is selling an aesthetic to people who want to look like they belong. We're making clothes for people who already belong — people who've been going to events for years and want apparel that reflects that depth of knowledge and commitment. The graphics reference things that casual observers wouldn't recognize.

How do you work with the underground community to develop products?

Every new product goes through a feedback process with regulars from the events we attend. We show up to events, talk to people about what they're wearing and why, and use that input directly in the design process. The community isn't a target market; it's our design partner.
