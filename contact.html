Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get featured on KEEPITIL?
Submit through our application form. We review all submissions and reach out if there's a fit. We're particularly interested in OC and LA-based artists, brands, and organizers playing underground electronic music — house, techno, drum and bass, garage, and related genres.
Is there a cost to be featured?
No. KEEPITIL does not charge for editorial coverage. If you're featured in our artist roster, brand directory, or organizer listings, it's because we think your work is worth covering — full stop.
Do you cover events outside OC and LA?
Our primary focus is Orange County and Los Angeles, including the Inland Empire and San Diego when there's a direct connection to the local scene. We occasionally cover national and international events when they directly feature the OC/LA community.
Can I submit a correction to my profile?
Yes — email us with the subject line "Profile Correction" and include the page URL and what needs to be updated. We try to keep profiles accurate and current.
Do you accept advertising?
Display advertising is served through Google AdSense. We do not offer direct ad placements or sponsored editorial content — our coverage remains independent from advertising relationships.
