PHANTOM

Deep Techno and Hardware Sessions from Orange County

📍 Garden Grove, CA 🎵 Deep Techno / Hypnotic / Hardware ⭐ KEEPITIL Artist

PHANTOM has been one of Orange County's most mysterious and consistent deep techno presences — no press, no social media self-promotion, just relentless shows and a growing reputation among the people who've been in the rooms. Garden Grove-based and fiercely underground, PHANTOM represents the techno DJ as craftsperson rather than personality.

"The machine is honest in a way humans rarely are — that's what I hear in the best techno."
THE STORY
You have almost no social media presence — how does that work in 2026?
Word of mouth still works if the music is good enough. Every booking I've gotten has come from someone who saw a set and told a promoter, or a promoter who came to a show and reached out. That chain of recommendation is slower than Instagram, but it produces better shows because the promoters who book me already know what they're getting. I don't think a social media presence would change the music, and the music is the point.
Garden Grove is KEEPITIL territory — how does the local community around you influence your sound?
The OC underground is tight-knit in a way that demands you actually care about what you're doing. There's no hiding in a small scene. The promoters know every DJ, the regulars know every set, and reputation is built event by event over years, not through viral moments. That environment creates a certain kind of artist — someone who develops slowly and sustainably rather than bursting and burning out.
THE CRAFT
Deep techno has a reputation for being inaccessible. How do you approach programming to hold a mixed room?
I don't try to make deep techno accessible — I try to find rooms and audiences that are already open to it. The mistake is trying to dilute the music to fit an audience that isn't there for it. The people who love deep techno are passionate about it in a way that casual listeners aren't, and playing for that core audience produces better events than trying to convert everyone.
What does the techno underground in OC need more of right now?
Venues with proper sound systems. The OC underground is full of people who take the music seriously but the physical infrastructure for events hasn't kept pace with the community's ambitions. A proper small techno venue in OC — 200 capacity, serious sound, committed programming — would change the scene significantly.
