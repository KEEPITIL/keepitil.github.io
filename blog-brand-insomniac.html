Insomniac Events — The Company That Built the SoCal Scene

📅 June 22, 2026 ✍ KEEPITIL Editorial 🎵 OC & LA Underground

The story of Insomniac Events is inseparable from the story of the SoCal electronic music scene. Pasquale Rotella started throwing parties in 1992 when the LA rave scene was still operating entirely outside official channels. Thirty-plus years later, Insomniac is the largest EDM event company in the world.

The Origin

The first Insomniac event drew a few hundred people to a warehouse. The vibe was PLUR, the music was mostly acid house and techno, and the entire operation ran on passion rather than infrastructure. That DNA — the emphasis on community, on wonder, on experience over profit — defined what the brand became.

“We were building something that didn’t have a name yet. We just knew it mattered.”

The EDC Legacy

Electric Daisy Carnival started as a modest outdoor event and grew into the defining festival of the American EDM generation. The 2010 move to Las Vegas after the LA tragedy that led to stricter permitting changed everything — and created the template for the modern mega-festival.

The Relationship with the Underground

Insomniac’s relationship with the underground is complicated. The company built its brand on underground culture, then scaled it into something the underground sometimes rejects. Their more intimate event lines — Factory 93, Bassrush — are genuine attempts to maintain roots. Whether they succeed depends on who you ask.
