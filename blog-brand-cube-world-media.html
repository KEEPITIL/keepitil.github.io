Cube World Media — OC’s Underground Event Production Company

📅 June 17, 2026 ✍ KEEPITIL Editorial 🎵 OC & LA Underground

Production companies rarely get the spotlight. They set up the sound, hang the lights, run the cables, and disappear before the crowd arrives. Cube World Media is one of the companies making OC events sound and look as good as they do.

What They Do

Cube World Media handles full event production — PA systems, subwoofer placement, DJ booth setup, lighting rigs, LED panels, and event logistics. They work with both large-format events and intimate underground club nights, adapting their approach to what the space requires.

“The crowd doesn’t notice good production. They just feel good in the room. That’s the point.”

The Technical Edge

What separates professional production from amateur is attention to details most attendees can’t articulate but definitely feel — sub bass optimization for the room, monitor placement for DJs, stage sightlines. Cube World Media gets these details right.

Working with Cube World

If you’re running events in OC and want production that elevates the experience, Cube World Media is the name in the underground. Follow on Instagram for their portfolio and contact info.
