About Merge the Music

Merge the Music is an OC-based multi-genre underground collective dedicated to creating events that bridge musical genres and communities. Based in Huntington Beach, Merge brings together house, techno, bass, and hip-hop fans under one roof for eclectic underground experiences.

The collective is known for its inclusive approach — every Merge event is designed to feel welcoming to newcomers and veterans alike, fostering a sense of community that goes beyond the music.

What They Do

Multi-genre underground event production, community building, artist showcases, and OC scene development.

Upcoming Events

Merge the Music hosts events throughout Orange County. Follow @mergethemusic for upcoming lineups and venue announcements.

Q & A
What genres does Merge the Music cover?
True to the name — everything. House, techno, bass, hip-hop, and more. They're committed to cross-genre vibes.
How long has Merge been active?
Merge the Music has been part of the OC underground scene for several years, growing a dedicated local following.
