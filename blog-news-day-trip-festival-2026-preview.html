Day Trip Festival 2026 — Preview and What to Expect This Weekend

📅 June 23, 2026 ✍ KEEPITIL Editorial 🎵 OC & LA Underground

Day Trip Festival arrives at Queen Mary Waterfront this weekend (June 27–28) with the strongest lineup they’ve assembled in years. The open-air waterfront setting has proven itself as one of the best festival footprints in SoCal — good sightlines, ocean breeze, and a crowd that comes specifically for house and techno.

The Lineup

Cloonee, GORDO, Odd Mob, Green Velvet B2B Josh Baker, Purple Disco Machine, Adam Beyer, Loco Dice, SIDEPIECE B2B Westend, and HI-LO headline across the weekend, with 50+ artists on multiple stages. This is a legitimate lineup for a festival of this scale — not a filler-heavy bill.

Day Trip built their reputation on curatorial integrity. This lineup justifies that reputation.

What to Know Before You Go

The venue is 21+ all weekend. Weather is expected to be warm with afternoon temperatures in the high 70s, dropping through the evening. Pack a light layer for late sets. The Queen Mary Waterfront location means parking logistics — plan for rideshare or arrive early. Tickets at daytripfestival.com.

Friday Pre-Party

The official Day Trip pre-party is tonight (June 26) at Exchange LA — the intimate warehouse setting is a significantly different experience than the outdoor festival and worth attending if you can add a third day to the weekend.
