How to Book Underground DJs: A Guide for Event Organizers

KEEPITIL Desk May 26, 2026 Tips & Guides · OC & LA Underground

Booking artists is the core creative decision in event production. Who you book signals what kind of event you're running, what audience you're building, and what you believe the music should do. Here's how to do it in the OC and LA underground without burning bridges or wasting money.

Underground Artist Rates in 2026

Local underground artists in OC and LA — artists who are actively building their careers but haven't broken into national touring — typically earn $100–500 per set at underground events. Artists with significant SoundCloud followings, festival credits, or a proven ability to draw crowds can earn $500–1,500 for local bookings. National touring artists visiting for a specific event start at $1,500 and go up significantly from there.

These numbers are rough guidelines — every booking is a negotiation based on the event's scale, the artist's relationship to the promoter, and what both parties think is fair for the specific context.

The Rider Conversation

Underground artists typically have minimal riders compared to commercial bookings: a set time, a plus-one, drinks, and sometimes accommodation if they're traveling. Ask about rider expectations clearly when confirming a booking — assumptions lead to uncomfortable conversations night-of.

What Artists Actually WantBeyond payment, most underground artists care about set time (not going on at 8PM to an empty room), sound quality, and being treated with basic respect by the promoter and venue staff. These are cheap to provide and make enormous differences in artist relationships.

Building a Local Artist Network

The best event programming comes from genuine relationships with artists rather than transactional bookings. Promoters who go to other promoters' events, tip artists they can't afford yet, and engage with the scene as participants — not just as business operators — build the kind of network that produces great lineups. The OC and LA underground is small enough that reputation travels fast.
