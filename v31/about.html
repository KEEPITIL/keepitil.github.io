ABOUT KEEPITIL

This isn't a brand. It's a movement.

A creator ecosystem — built to help creators grow. Born in the OC & LA underground, open to every genre and craft.

What we are

KEEPITIL is a creator ecosystem. Our mission is simple: help creators grow. We give artists, organizers, venues and brands a home for their work, a way to reach their people, and the tools to build something that lasts — profiles, events and ticketing, a community feed, and original coverage of the culture around it.

Events are one part of it, not the whole thing. We started in Southern California's underground electronic scene — that's our origin, not our ceiling. Hip-hop, country, jazz, rock, latin, afrobeats, comedy, art, film, gaming: if you make something and want an audience for it, this is built for you.

We started because independent creators deserve their own platform. Not aggregated listings, not algorithmic feeds, not the same ten globally-famous names — but real support for the people making something genuine happen in their own cities.

What we cover

  • Artists & creators — profiles and interviews across every genre and craft, from established names to the next generation of local talent.
  • Brands — spotlights on the labels, clothing lines, gear shops, and creative studios building the infrastructure behind the culture.
  • Organizers & venues — the promoters, collectives and spaces putting on events of every kind: shows, club nights, markets, screenings and community gatherings.
  • Culture — long-form editorial on history, scene dynamics, craft and technique, and the business of being an independent creator.

Where we are

We go deep locally before we go wide. Southern California is home base — from Long Beach to DTLA, from Anaheim to Riverside, from the beach cities to the San Fernando Valley — because local scenes are better served by dedicated attention than by brief mentions in platforms chasing national reach. That's the model we intend to carry city by city.

Editorial independence

KEEPITIL does not take payment for coverage. Being featured in our creator roster, brand directory, or organizer listings is based on editorial merit — we reach out to people whose work we find genuinely interesting, and we accept applications from the community through our open submission process.

The name

"Keep It IL" is a phrase from the culture — shorthand for keeping things authentic and independent, outside the commercial mainstream. It started in dance music as a marker of real: real work, real community, real scene. It applies to every creator who means it.

Want to be featured? We accept applications from creators, brands, organizers and venues. Tell us what you make, and we'll take it from there.
