Bass Music Deep Cuts — OC & LA Producers You’re Not Listening to Yet

📅 June 13, 2026 ✍ KEEPITIL Editorial 🎵 OC & LA Underground

The algorithm rewards what’s already popular. Underground bass music rarely is — until it suddenly is. These are the producers operating below that threshold who are making genuinely compelling music right now, in 2026, in the OC and LA underground.

Bedroom Producers Worth Finding

Some of the most interesting bass music in the SoCal scene is coming from producers who haven’t started playing live regularly yet. Bandcamp and SoundCloud remain the best places to find this material before it gets aggregated by the playlisting machines.

The basement producers of 2026 are the headliners of 2028. Start listening now.

The OC Bass Pipeline

Orange County has developed a genuine bass music pipeline — emerging artists getting stage time at Bass Mafia OC and OC Rave Collective events before moving up to larger LA stages. These events are the best place to discover who’s coming next.

The IE Wild Card

Don’t sleep on the Inland Empire. The IE underground is producing bass music with a rawness that OC and LA events often lack. Less polished production, more genuine energy. Worth the drive.

How to Find It

Follow KEEPITIL on Instagram, check our events calendar, and actually show up to local events. The best way to find deep cut bass music is to be in the room where it’s being played.
