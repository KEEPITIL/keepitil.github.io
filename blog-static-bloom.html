STATIC BLOOM

Electronica and Leftfield Bass from Los Angeles

📍 Los Feliz, CA 🎵 Electronica / Leftfield / Bass Music ⭐ KEEPITIL Artist

Static Bloom occupies a deliberately uncategorizable position in the LA underground — sets that move through electronica, leftfield bass, IDM-adjacent textures, and moments of genuine dance floor impact without committing to any single genre identity. Los Feliz-based and increasingly sought after for the kinds of events that want something that can't be summarized in two words.

"The best electronic music creates a space you didn't know you needed to inhabit."

THE STORY
'Leftfield' is a genre label that resists definition — how do you describe what you play to someone who hasn't heard it?
Electronic music made by people who cared more about what was interesting than what was functional. The best leftfield music has the same emotional directness as the best pop music and the same structural sophistication as the best contemporary classical music, but it exists outside the distribution and marketing systems of either. When I play it in a room, I'm trusting the audience to follow me somewhere unfamiliar.
Los Feliz puts you in a specific LA creative community — how does that environment shape the work?
Los Feliz has this long history as a home for LA's creative underground — writers, filmmakers, musicians who aren't chasing mainstream success. Living in that environment means my music doesn't have to justify itself commercially, and that freedom shows up in the selections. I'm more willing to play something that might challenge the room if the neighborhood around me values the challenge.

THE CRAFT
How do you handle a crowd that came expecting something more functional and encounters your sets?
With patience and without apology. I trust that if I'm playing music I believe in, and I'm sequencing it well, the room will follow. And if they don't, I've learned something about the mismatch between myself and that context. The shows where people are slightly disoriented at first and then completely absorbed by the end are the ones I value most.
Where do you see your sound going in 2026?
More original production in the leftfield space, and more connections with the experimental music community in LA that exists parallel to the club world. I want to play more events that don't fit neatly into the club format — gallery openings, installation spaces, events where the sonic environment is the whole point rather than a support for something else.
