10 Rising SoCal Artists to Watch in 2026

KEEPITIL Desk June 6, 2026 Artist Feature · OC & LA Underground

The SoCal underground is bigger than its press coverage suggests. Here are ten artists who've been building real careers away from mainstream attention — with roots in OC, LA, the IE, and SD — and who are positioned to define the scene's next chapter.

RAB3L — OC/Torrance

KEEPITIL's first featured artist for good reason. RAB3L has been DJing since childhood and brings a UKG and bass music fluency to the OC scene that's genuinely rare. His recorded mixes demonstrate both technical control and musical taste. Read the full profile →

AZTEKAN — San Bernardino/IE

Hypnotic deep techno from the Inland Empire. AZTEKAN's sets are built for long rooms and patient crowds — the kind of music that reveals itself slowly and rewards attention. Read the full profile →

LVNKY BONEZ — LA

LA-based dubstep and DnB producer whose original tracks have been building buzz on SoundCloud. "Captain Crunch" established his production voice early. Read the full profile →

PYPRR — Los Angeles

LA-based and building a reputation on the underground circuit for technically precise sets with musical range. Watch the shows she's booking in 2026. Read the full profile →

CHLOPHONIC — Santa Ana/OC

OC bass and house from Santa Ana with a sound influenced by both Southern California's diverse musical culture and the UK bass music tradition. Read the full profile →

CROOKI3 — Riverside

Riverside-based artist connecting IE and OC scenes with sets that move between house, bass, and techno without feeling scattered. Read the full profile →

SMOKE — OC

OC underground veteran who's been in the scene long enough to have context for what's happening now. Consistently reliable sets and a growing reputation beyond his home region. Read the full profile →

TRANCEONE — Los Angeles

LA-based with a sound that bridges trance influences and contemporary club music. One of the more unique sonic positions in the current LA underground. Read the full profile →

BB SHAINE — Los Angeles

LA artist with bass music roots and a growing presence on the underground circuit. One to watch for set quality and musical development in 2026. Read the full profile →

GROOVE TROOPER — Garden Grove/OC

Latin tech house from Garden Grove with Guatemalan roots and a track record that includes Groove Cruise Miami and Avalon LA. The most internationally experienced artist on this list. Read the full profile →
