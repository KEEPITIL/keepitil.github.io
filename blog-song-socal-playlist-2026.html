10 SoCal Artists You Need on Your Playlist Right Now

📅 June 19, 2026 ✍ KEEPITIL Editorial 🎵 OC & LA Underground

The SoCal underground produces more music than most people realize. The problem isn’t a lack of output — it’s that the distribution channels that surface local underground music are inconsistent. Here are ten artists actively releasing material in 2026 that should be in your regular rotation.

Bass and Electronic

BB Shaine (Bass/UKG), Lvnky Bonez (Dubstep), DSA (Bass production) — these three are consistently releasing music that represents the SoCal bass scene at its most creative. Bass House with genuine groove, dubstep that favors atmosphere over aggression, and bass production that defies easy categorization.

House and Techno

SWERVE, Oscar Osorio, KriStyle, and RAB3L are making house and techno that sounds like Southern California — warm but hard, groove-oriented but technically accomplished. These are the tracks your playlist needs if it currently consists entirely of European techno.

The best local music sounds like where it’s from. SoCal has a sound. Start listening for it.

Experimental and Left-Field

GalacticGumby (Speed Garage), Demek (Industrial Techno), and NOCTUA (Dark Ambient Techno) represent the more adventurous edge of the local scene. These are the artists who are genuinely pushing the sound into territory that the mainstream hasn’t caught up with yet.

How to Support Local Artists

Buy their music on Bandcamp. Show up to their events. Follow their socials and actually engage. Share their music with people you know. The underground runs on real support, not algorithm optimization.
