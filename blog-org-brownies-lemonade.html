About Brownies & Lemonade

Brownies & Lemonade is a Los Angeles-based event collective known for their open-format, community-first approach to underground events. What started as a college party series evolved into one of LA's most respected underground brands, blending electronic, hip-hop, and bass music with a laid-back California vibe.

The B&L brand has grown to include record releases, merchandise, and artist management while remaining true to their community roots. They've hosted hundreds of events across LA and beyond, giving early platforms to artists who have gone on to mainstream success.

What They Do

Open-format event production, record releases, artist management, brand collaborations, and underground cultural events throughout Los Angeles.

Upcoming Events

B&L hosts events regularly across LA venues. Follow @browniesandlemonade for upcoming dates, pop-ups, and surprise announcements.

Q & A
What genre is Brownies & Lemonade?
Truly open-format — they blend electronic, hip-hop, bass music, and whatever sounds good. No walls between genres.
Have any major artists come up through B&L?
Yes — many artists who played early B&L events have gone on to headline major festivals. They have a track record of spotting talent.
