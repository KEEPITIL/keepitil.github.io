About Another Planet Entertainment

Another Planet Entertainment (APE) is the Bay Area's leading independent concert promotion and festival production company. APE is responsible for producing Outside Lands Music and Arts Festival in Golden Gate Park — one of the country's most beloved urban music festivals — along with hundreds of concerts throughout the Bay Area annually.

Founded in 2003, APE has become synonymous with high-quality live music experiences in Northern California, operating venues like the Fox Oakland and producing events at venues throughout the Bay Area.

What They Do

Festival production (Outside Lands), concert promotion, venue management (Fox Oakland), and Bay Area live music events.

Upcoming Events

Outside Lands takes place annually in August at Golden Gate Park, San Francisco. APE produces year-round concerts throughout the Bay Area. Visit apeconcerts.com for full calendar.
