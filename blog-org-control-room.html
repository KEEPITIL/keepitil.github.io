About Control Room

Control Room is one of the collectives at the heart of Los Angeles' warehouse rave revival — a crew co-founded by Shelbie DeJesus and Jesse Joshua, who have been part of the city's underground since they were teenagers and who also produce techno together as White Lotus. The two met through the scene's grapevine, bonded over a shared love of shuffling, built a dance crew, and grew it into a community and an events operation.

Like many of LA's best warehouse organizers, Control Room isn't driven by money — it's driven by a genuine love of the culture and the people in it. Their events center hard, hypnotic techno in raw industrial spaces, the kind of rooms where the sound system and the crowd matter more than the bottle service.

What They Do

Warehouse techno rave production, community building, and a steady platform for the DJs and dancers who define LA's underground. Control Room is part of the wider ecosystem of femme- and community-led crews keeping the city's cutting-edge club culture alive outside the festival machine.

Upcoming Events

Control Room hosts warehouse techno events across Los Angeles. As with most underground productions, locations are typically shared close to event day for security. Follow @controlroom.la for lineups and announcements.

Q & A
What genre does Control Room focus on?
Techno — hard, hypnotic, warehouse-driven. Their events lean into the raw, industrial end of the spectrum that defines LA's underground.
Who is behind Control Room?
Co-founders Shelbie DeJesus and Jesse Joshua, longtime members of the LA scene who also produce techno as White Lotus. Control Room grew out of a dance crew and a shared love of the culture.
How do I find out about their events?
Follow @controlroom.la on Instagram. Like most warehouse productions, venues are usually announced shortly before the event.
