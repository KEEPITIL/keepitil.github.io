ABOUT 6AM GROUP

6AM Group is a Los Angeles-based underground rave community, event promoter, and electronic music media platform. Built around the city's techno and house scene, 6AM covers underground electronic music culture — its artists, technology, and lifestyle — while also producing its own warehouse-style events across LA.

What sets 6AM apart from a straightforward promoter is its dual identity: it functions as both a media outlet, publishing interviews, news, and features on the global underground, and as a working events brand that puts LA dancers in front of touring and local techno talent. That combination has made 6AM one of the more recognizable independent names in the Southern California underground.

WHAT THEY DO
Warehouse and venue techno events across Los Angeles
Editorial coverage of underground electronic music
Artist interviews, premieres, and scene reporting
Community-building around LA's techno and house culture

WHY THEY MATTER

A healthy underground needs more than parties — it needs documentation. 6AM Group fills both roles at once, throwing the kind of techno nights that keep LA's warehouse tradition going while building an archive of the scene through its media platform. For artists trying to break into the LA underground, it represents both a stage and a megaphone.

KEEPITIL features 6AM Group as part of mapping the organizers shaping Southern California's underground electronic music landscape.
