About Neon District Lights

Neon District Lights is an Anaheim-based event lighting and visual effects company specializing in underground raves, warehouse events, and electronic music productions throughout Orange County and Southern California. From LED arrays to laser systems, Neon District creates the visual atmosphere that elevates any underground event.

The team brings years of experience in underground event production, understanding the specific needs of rave and electronic music environments — including full blackout setup, rigging, and on-site technical support.

What They Offer

Event lighting design and installation, laser systems, LED setups, visual effects production, and technical support for underground events throughout OC and SoCal.

LED lighting arrays and wash lights
Laser systems and haze machines
Pixel mapping and visual programming
On-site technical support and breakdown
