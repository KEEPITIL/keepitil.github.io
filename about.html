About KEEPITIL

Independent underground electronic music coverage for Orange County & Los Angeles.

What We Are

KEEPITIL is an independent editorial platform dedicated to the underground electronic music community of Southern California. We cover the artists, promoters, brands, and events that define the OC and LA underground — the warehouse raves, the late-night clubs, the record shops, the local labels, and the communities that keep the scene alive without waiting for mainstream validation.

We started because the underground deserves its own media. Not aggregated festival coverage, not algorithmically-generated playlists, not listicles of the same ten globally-famous names — but real, original writing about the music and people making something genuine happen in our cities.

"The underground is where it starts. We're here to document it before it disappears."

Our Coverage Area

KEEPITIL is focused on two of the most vibrant underground electronic music scenes in the United States: Orange County and Los Angeles. From Long Beach to DTLA, from Anaheim to Riverside, from the beach cities to the San Fernando Valley — if it's happening in Southern California's underground, we're paying attention.

Our geographic focus is a deliberate editorial choice. There's more than enough going on locally to fill a publication entirely — and local scenes are better served by dedicated coverage than by brief mentions in publications chasing national or global reach.

Editorial Independence

KEEPITIL does not take payment for coverage. Being featured in our artist roster, brand directory, or organizer listings is based on editorial merit — we reach out to people whose work we find genuinely interesting, and we accept applications from the scene through our open submission process.

The Site does display advertising through Google AdSense to support its operation. Advertising is sold through the Google AdSense network and does not influence editorial decisions.

The Name

"Keep It IL" is a phrase from the culture — a shorthand for keeping things underground, authentic, and outside the commercial mainstream. IL (Illinois, originally from Chicago house and drill culture) has been adopted broadly across dance music scenes as a marker of real: real music, real communities, real nights. KEEPITIL is a commitment to that standard for the Southern California underground.
