DRIFTER

Deep House and Organic Sounds from Orange County

📍 Costa Mesa, CA 🎵 Deep House / Organic House / Melodic Techno ⭐ KEEPITIL Artist

DRIFTER has been one of Orange County's most consistent deep house presences since 2020 — sets built on organic textures, melodic progression, and the kind of patient groove that distinguishes deep house from its more percussive relatives. Costa Mesa born, OC committed, and increasingly present on LA's underground circuit as the two scenes continue to converge.

"The groove has to be honest before anything else — technical perfection means nothing without emotional truth."
THE STORY
Deep house occupies a different emotional register than most of the underground — what draws you to that space?
Deep house is about the journey rather than the destination. Where techno builds toward a peak and releases it, deep house maintains a sustained emotional state — sometimes melancholic, sometimes euphoric, but always introspective. The best deep house sets create a kind of shared inner life for everyone in the room, and that's harder to achieve and more rewarding when you get there.
Orange County's underground scene has historically leaned toward heavier sounds — how does deep house fit in?
OC's underground is more genre-diverse than the stereotype. There's a community of listeners who've been hungry for the deeper, more melodic end of the electronic music spectrum, and my shows have found that audience. Costa Mesa and Huntington Beach have strong surf and alternative music cultures that bleed into an appreciation for atmospheric, emotional music — deep house fits that sensibility.
THE CRAFT
What's your approach to selecting tracks for a four-hour set versus a two-hour support slot?
A four-hour set is a completely different art form. In two hours, you're making an argument. In four hours, you're building a world. The early part of a long set is about establishing trust and setting atmosphere — playing beautifully without demanding too much of the room. The middle section is where you take risks. The final third is where you deliver on the emotional promise of everything that came before.
Who are the deep house artists that have most influenced your approach?
Larry Heard's Mr. Fingers records — the originals, not the remixes — are the foundation. Theo Parrish for understanding that deep house can be demanding and challenging while still being dance music. Floating Points for showing how a contemporary artist can extend the vocabulary. And locally, the artists who've been quietly building the OC deep music community without any industry attention deserve credit they don't always receive.
