ABOUT NEON DESERT

Neon Desert is a Riverside-based event collective that runs underground electronic music events across the Inland Empire and in the desert-edge communities east of the LA basin — territory that most SoCal promoters ignore and that Neon Desert has made into a distinctive circuit with its own character and community.

The collective's aesthetic is informed by its geography: the industrial landscape of the IE and the open desert spaces to the east create a visual and sonic context that makes Neon Desert events feel different from anything happening in LA or OC. Events happen in warehouses, desert properties, and unusual industrial spaces that the IE geography makes available.

Q&A
The IE underground gets very little press coverage — how do you build community without that visibility?

Through genuine relationship building. When you don't have press coverage, every attendee at every event is a word-of-mouth ambassador for what you're doing. We focus on making each event exceptional so that everyone who attends tells multiple people. Slow growth through genuine quality is more sustainable than viral moments.

What makes desert-edge events specifically interesting from an event production standpoint?

The acoustic environment is completely different from a warehouse or a club — you're working with open space, natural acoustics, and weather variables that don't exist in enclosed venues. The sound systems need to perform differently, the experience for attendees is physically different, and the atmosphere is unlike anything you can create in a conventional venue. It's more production work for more distinctive results.
