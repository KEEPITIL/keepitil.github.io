LIGHTLEAK

From Laguna Beach To The World's Floors

Laguna Beach, CA
Minimal · Deep Tech
KEEPITIL Artist

LIGHTLEAK is one of Orange County's clearest minimal and deep tech success stories. Raised in Laguna Beach, he built a sound out of rolling basslines, soulful vocal cuts, and the kind of stripped-back groove that fills a room without ever shouting. After catching the ear of tastemakers across the minimal scene, he made the bold move overseas to immerse himself in the European circuit — and in 2025 his debut EP "Shelter" landed on Hottrax, the London label founded by Jamie Jones, and shot to number one on Beatport's house chart. KEEPITIL features LIGHTLEAK as a SoCal-raised artist taking the local sound global.

Minimal and deep tech with feeling — infectious rhythms, soulful vocals, and rolling basslines built for the long stretch of the night.
THE SOUND
Minimal & deep tech
LIGHTLEAK works in the minimal and deep tech lane — hypnotic, low-slung grooves with just enough warmth to keep a floor moving for hours. The productions lean on infectious rhythm, soulful vocal touches, and rolling basslines rather than big-drop fireworks, a sound made for connoisseur rooms and after-hours energy.
Built for the deep end
It's the kind of music that rewards patience. Where some artists chase the peak, LIGHTLEAK builds momentum slowly and holds it, letting the groove do the work. That restraint is exactly what made his tracks travel from SoCal bedrooms to European club systems.
THE RESUME
"Shelter" on Hottrax
In 2025 LIGHTLEAK made his debut on Hottrax — the London label started by Jamie Jones in 2013 — with the three-track "Shelter" EP, featuring "Shelter (Funky Heroes)," "Memphis," and "Hamburg." The release reached number one on Beatport's house chart and drew support from BBC Radio 1 programmers.
Backed by the scene's tastemakers
His slick blend of minimal and deep tech caught the attention of artists like Prunk, Sidney Charles, and Rossi. before he relocated to Europe to go deeper into the scene — a Laguna Beach kid steadily turning a hometown sound into an international name.
Find the music
LIGHTLEAK's catalog lives across SoundCloud, Beatport, and the major streaming platforms, with new work surfacing first through Instagram — the best window into where the project heads next.
THE SCENE
OC roots, global reach
LIGHTLEAK is part of a generation of Southern California artists carrying the region's underground sound onto bigger stages. KEEPITIL features him as proof that a serious minimal and deep tech career can start on the OC coast and end up topping international charts.
