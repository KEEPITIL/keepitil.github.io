Festival Packing Guide — What to Bring to a SoCal Rave

📅 June 18, 2026 ✍ KEEPITIL Editorial 🎵 OC & LA Underground

SoCal summer events present specific packing challenges that generic festival lists don’t address: extreme heat during the day, surprising cold after midnight when the marine layer rolls in, and the physical demands of dancing for 6-10 hours in June-August heat.

The Non-Negotiables

Earplugs — high-fidelity ones, not foam. You will be standing near systems capable of sustained SPLs that cause permanent hearing damage. Decibullz, Loop, or Eargasm all make good options. Hydration: 500ml of water per hour is a minimum in summer heat. Sunscreen: SPF 50+, reapply every 90 minutes for outdoor events.

The only thing worse than missing a set is missing future sets because you damaged your hearing. Wear earplugs.

The Temperature Swing Problem

OC and LA summer events can hit 95°F at 3PM and 60°F at 1AM, especially near the coast. Pack a light layer (hoodie, flannel) in your bag and leave it there until you need it. A packable rain jacket also works as a wind layer and fits in a fanny pack.

The Fanny Pack Setup

Fanny pack or small crossbody is far superior to a backpack for dancing. Keep it light: ID, cards, phone, earplugs, lip balm, a small portable charger. Everything else can stay in your car or a locker.

Shoes

Shoes matter more than any other piece of clothing. Dancing for 8 hours in the wrong shoes causes real physical pain. Flat soles with adequate cushioning, broken in before the event. Platforms look great but hour 6 is not their friend.
