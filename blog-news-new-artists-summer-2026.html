New Artists Joining the KEEPITIL Roster — Summer 2026

📅 June 20, 2026 ✍ KEEPITIL Editorial 🎵 OC & LA Underground

The KEEPITIL roster grows every season as new talent emerges from the OC, LA, and IE underground scenes. Summer 2026 brings a strong new cohort — artists who have been building their presence through consistent performance and original production, now getting the platform they’ve earned.

What We Look For

KEEPITIL features artists who are genuinely embedded in the SoCal underground — people who show up, who support the scene, who play events that matter to the community. We’re not looking for the most followers; we’re looking for the most real.

The underground produces talent faster than it gets recognized. Our job is to close that gap.

New Additions This Summer

The summer 2026 additions include artists across the full genre spectrum — from hard techno and acid to deep house and bass music. The roster now covers a wider sonic range than ever, reflecting the genuine diversity of what’s happening in the SoCal underground scene.

Apply for the Roster

If you’re a DJ or producer based in OC, LA, or the IE and you’re active in the underground scene, apply at keepitil.com/apply. We review applications on a rolling basis and prioritize artists with genuine scene involvement over follower counts.
