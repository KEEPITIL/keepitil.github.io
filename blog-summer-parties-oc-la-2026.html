Summer 2026: The Parties You Cannot Miss in OC & LA

KEEPITIL Desk June 14, 2026 Los Angeles · Orange County

Summer in Southern California means the calendar shifts. Warehouses give way to open-air venues. Rooftop series launch. Waterfront stages appear. The indoor clubs that define the winter underground are still there, but the most memorable nights between June and September tend to happen outside — in parks, parking lots, beachside lots, and festival grounds where the bass carries further and the crowd spreads out under open sky.

This is our summer 2026 roundup: the events that represent the range of what's happening across OC and LA from June through September. Big productions and underground showcases, weekend festivals and weekly residencies, known venues and pop-up spaces. Not every event on this list is massive — some of the best ones aren't. What they share is programming worth your time and money.

Festivals & Major Productions

Day Trip Festival — Queen Mary Waterfront
June 27–28 · Long Beach · 21+ · House / Disco / Techno
Insomniac and Day Trip's flagship SoCal festival returns to the Queen Mary Waterfront in Long Beach for two days of house, disco and techno — anchored by a new 11,000-capacity Long Beach Amphitheater stage. Cloonee, Odd Mob, DJ Gordo, Green Velvet B2B Josh Baker, Purple Disco Machine and 50+ artists across the weekend. This is the centerpiece of the Southern California summer calendar. Read our full preview →
Summer of the Living Dead 2026 — Bassrush
June 19–20 · Queen Mary Waterfront, Long Beach · 21+ · Bass / Dubstep / D&B
Bassrush's annual flagship returns to the Queen Mary Waterfront for two days of heavy bass, dubstep, riddim and drum & bass. One of the most cinematically staged outdoor events in the SoCal bass calendar — and one of the few productions that can rival Day Trip for sheer spectacle. Read our full preview →
Lost in Dreams 2026
July · Los Angeles TBA · All Ages daytime / 21+ evening · Various Genres
Lost in Dreams has become one of the most eagerly anticipated annual events in the LA electronic calendar — a production that pairs serious musical programming with an immersive visual environment that transforms whatever space it occupies. Multi-stage, multi-genre, and consistently one of the better-curated lineups of the summer. Details via official channels as they drop.

Underground Showcases & Club Series

KEEPITIL Summer Showcase Series — OC & LA
Ongoing through September · Multiple Venues · 21+ · House / Tech House / Techno
KEEPITIL's summer showcase series brings together artists from the KEEPITIL roster — including RAB3L, Groove Trooper, LVNKY BONES, and SWERVE — for a series of events across Orange County and Los Angeles. Intimate crowds, serious programming, and artists you'll be watching in much bigger rooms in 12 months. Watch the Events page for confirmed dates and venues as they lock.
Factory 93 Summer Series — Exchanged LA & Outdoor Spaces
July–September · Los Angeles · 21+ · Techno / Underground House
Insomniac's underground techno brand runs its most consistent LA programming during summer months — anchoring Exchange LA for monthly events while also activating outdoor spaces for larger-format shows. Factory 93 events are the highest-production underground techno experiences accessible in the LA market. Check the Factory 93 Instagram for monthly drops.
Santa Ana Arts District Pop-Ups
Every weekend, June–August · Arts District, Santa Ana · 21+ · All Underground Genres
The most consistently active pop-up zone in OC during summer months. Lot 579 and surrounding warehouse spaces in the Santa Ana Arts District host events nearly every weekend — announced 7–14 days out via promoter Instagram accounts. Follow OC-based promoters closely; this is where OC's underground actually lives when the weather is good.

What to Know Before You Go

Summer events in LA and OC come with a few consistent logistics worth building into your planning: outdoor events typically have earlier cutoffs than indoor clubs (midnight to 1am for festival-format events), dress codes at indoor clubs run strict June through September as venue traffic peaks, and parking at waterfront venues like the Queen Mary books out — arrive early or plan for rideshare. Buy tickets in advance for anything on this list that has them; summer events at this level regularly sell out.

"The best summer nights in the SoCal underground aren't the ones you planned six weeks out. They're the ones the Santa Ana promoters announced on Tuesday for Saturday."
