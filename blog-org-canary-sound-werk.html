About Canary Sound Werk

Canary Sound Werk is one of the rare permanent warehouse spaces in Los Angeles — a fixed home for a scene that usually lives in temporary, last-minute locations. Set in the heart of the city's Fashion District, it runs out of Canary's exhibition headquarters and brings together a finely tuned in-house sound system with a rotating cast of LA's finest underground crews, including Spiraling, Pranks, Serenity Link and Auntie.

The space comes alive with bass-heavy, dubbed-out sounds from some of the city's sharpest DJs, fostering communal experiences while elevating local talent and showcasing boundary-pushing musicians from around the world. A monthly radio show on dublab extends those sounds beyond the warehouse walls, and proceeds help support the connected Los Angeles exhibition program.

What They Do

Warehouse events, a permanent in-house rig, a monthly dublab radio residency, and a platform for the experimental, bass-driven end of LA's underground. As an art-and-music space rather than a commercial club, Canary Sound Werk operates squarely outside the festival machine — a place built around sound and community first.

Q & A
What makes Canary Sound Werk different?
It's one of the few permanent warehouse spaces in LA, with its own tuned in-house sound system — most of the city's warehouse scene runs out of temporary, reveal-day locations.
What kind of sound do they program?
Bass-heavy, dubbed-out and experimental — a rotating cast of LA crews like Spiraling, Pranks, Serenity Link and Auntie, plus boundary-pushing DJs from around the world.
How do I find out about their events?
