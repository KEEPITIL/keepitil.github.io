CASPER GREY

Tech House and Club Culture from Orange County

📍 Anaheim, CA 🎵 Tech House / House / Club ⭐ KEEPITIL Artist

Casper Grey has been a consistent presence in the OC club and underground circuit since 2019 — tech house sets built for movement, informed by the specific demographics and musical culture of Anaheim and the surrounding OC geography. Club-ready but with genuine underground roots, Casper Grey bridges the worlds that most artists have to choose between.

"The best tech house sets feel inevitable in hindsight — every track in exactly the right place."

THE STORY
Anaheim is Disney, Angels baseball, and suburban sprawl — not exactly where you'd expect an underground electronic music career to develop.
And that's exactly why the underground there is special. When the mainstream culture of a place is so sanitized and commercial, the people who want something real build it underground. The warehouse and loft events I came up through were populated by people who were specifically rejecting the polished entertainment culture around them. That gives an underground scene a kind of intensity and intentionality you don't always find in markets where the mainstream has more texture.
Tech house has become massive commercially — does that change how you approach it?
I play the music that I love and that works in the rooms I play, and I don't spend much energy on what the genre label means at the industry level. The tech house conversation has gotten complicated by its commercial success, but the actual music — when it's good — is about groove and drive and the specific pleasure of a well-built four-on-the-floor track. That hasn't changed.

THE CRAFT
How do you distinguish your sets from the thousands of other tech house DJs working right now?
Selection and sequencing over novelty. I'm not trying to play tracks that nobody else has — I'm trying to build a set that moves differently from any other set someone could hear that night. The same track in a different position in a different context can feel completely new. Understanding that is the difference between DJing as playlist execution and DJing as musical performance.
What's next for you in 2026?
More shows across OC and LA, working on some original productions that I'm hoping to release through a local label connection, and continuing to build the network of artists and promoters that makes this scene function. The music is the reason, but the community is what sustains it.
