About HARD Events

HARD Events is a Los Angeles-based festival production company known for HARD Summer, HARD Day of the Dead, and other large-scale electronic music events. Founded by Gary Richards (a.k.a. Destructo), HARD brought an edgier, more rock-influenced aesthetic to LA's festival scene when it launched in the 2000s.

HARD is now part of Live Nation's portfolio, producing massive multi-stage festivals at venues like the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino. The brand is known for booking an eclectic mix of electronic, hip-hop, and indie acts.

What They Do

Multi-day festival production, artist booking, and large-scale event management throughout Southern California.

Upcoming Events

HARD Summer takes place annually in Southern California in late summer. HARD Day of the Dead runs near Halloween. Visit hardfest.com for tickets and lineup info.

Q & A
What makes HARD different from other festivals?
HARD combines electronic music with hip-hop and alternative acts, creating a genre-blending atmosphere unique in the festival space.
Where are HARD festivals held?
Primarily at the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino, CA, with occasional events at other SoCal venues.
