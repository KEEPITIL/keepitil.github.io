About ProDJ Supply

ProDJ Supply is a Los Angeles-based DJ equipment store and rental company serving professional DJs, event organizers, and producers throughout Southern California. Stocking professional-grade gear from Pioneer, Denon, Rane, and all major DJ equipment brands, ProDJ is the go-to source for working DJ equipment in LA.

Beyond retail, ProDJ Supply offers equipment rental for events, DJ booth setup services, and expert consultation for DJs looking to upgrade their setup. Their staff are working DJs who understand equipment from the inside out.

What They Offer

DJ equipment sales, gear rental, DJ booth setup and configuration, technical support, and consultation for DJs and event organizers in Los Angeles.
