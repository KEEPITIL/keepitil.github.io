About DIRTYBIRD Records

DIRTYBIRD Records is a San Francisco-based house music record label founded by DJ Claude VonStroke in 2005. Known for its quirky, bass-driven take on house music and deep techno, DIRTYBIRD became one of the most beloved independent labels in electronic music, releasing music by artists like Justin Martin, J.Phlip, Ardalan, and many others.

Beyond music releases, DIRTYBIRD produces the annual DIRTYBIRD Campout festival — an overnight camping festival in the woods of Northern California that has become one of the underground music community's most cherished events, blending music, games, and community in a unique outdoor setting.

What They Offer

Music releases (house, techno, bass), DIRTYBIRD Campout festival production, artist development, and community events for the DIRTYBIRD family.
