DIRTYBIRD Records — How Claude VonStroke Built the House Underground

📅 June 19, 2026 ✍ KEEPITIL Editorial 🎵 OC & LA Underground

DIRTYBIRD Records launched in 2005 with a simple premise: house music didn’t have to be serious. It could be weird, funny, irreverent, and still hit hard on the dance floor. That premise, executed by Barclay Crenshaw (aka Claude VonStroke), produced one of the most influential independent labels in house music history.

The Sound

DIRTYBIRD’s aesthetic is a specific blend: deep, rolling basslines; off-kilter percussion; vocal samples that veer between catchy and absurd; a production quality that sounds simultaneously lo-fi and perfectly crafted. It’s house music that doesn’t take itself too seriously but takes the dance floor deadly seriously.

“The name says it all. We wanted to make music that felt raw and real.”

The DIRTYBIRD Campout

The DIRTYBIRD Campout has become one of the most beloved events in underground house music — a camping festival that embodies the label’s community-first ethos. The event feels like a family reunion for people who found their family through music.

The LA Connection

DIRTYBIRD has maintained a strong presence in the SoCal market, with label nights at key LA venues and a fanbase that runs deep in the house community across OC and LA.
