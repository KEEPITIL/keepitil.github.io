RAB3L — Built From the Booth, Raised by Sound

Some DJs grow into the craft. Others are born into it. RAB3L is the latter.

KEEPITIL

Some DJs grow into the craft. Others are born into it. RAB3L is the latter.

Hailing from Torrance, California, Bryan Ricardo Mentado — known behind the decks as RAB3L — has been immersed in DJ culture since the age of eight. Not as a hobby, not as a phase, but as part of his DNA. With roots tied directly to family influence, music wasn’t something he discovered. It was something he inherited, studied, and mastered over time.

That early start didn’t just give him experience. It gave him instinct. And that instinct is exactly what separates RAB3L from the wave of emerging DJs flooding the scene today.

A sound that reads the room

RAB3L operates in the high-energy lanes of bass house, tech house, UKG, and bassline — but labelling his sound alone doesn’t fully capture the experience. What truly defines him is his ability to read a room and respond in real time.

Where some DJs rely on pre-planned sets, RAB3L leans into years of hands-on experience and musical awareness. His sets are fluid, reactive, and deeply intentional. Whether it’s a packed club or an underground crowd, he knows how to shift energy, build momentum, and deliver moments that stick.

Expect bouncy rhythms. Festival-level euphoria. Groovy transitions that keep bodies moving and heads locked in. No matter the crowd, he adapts, elevates, and delivers.

Proven where it counts

While many artists are still chasing their breakout moment, RAB3L has already stepped into notable rooms like Avalon Hollywood and Academy LA — two venues known for demanding crowds and high standards.

He’s also made his mark through events like Utopia and AfterMidnight.LA, consistently proving he can hold his own on serious stages.

And if you ask him what track never fails? “The Days” by Notion — a UKG staple that perfectly reflects his taste: rhythmic, infectious, and built for movement.

Built different: experience meets preparation

Ask what makes him stand out, and RAB3L points to one thing — knowledge.

Years of gigs, technical understanding of DJ equipment, and constant preparation give him an edge that isn’t easy to replicate. He’s not just pressing play. He’s crafting an experience with intention.

Behind the scenes, that same discipline shows up in his routine. Before every set he centres himself with words of affirmation — a small but powerful ritual that keeps him grounded and focused before stepping into high-energy environments.

More than music

Outside of music, RAB3L works as a technician for Chevy — another layer that speaks to his technical mindset and precision. It’s that balance between discipline and creativity that shapes how he approaches his craft.

And when the night ends? You’ll probably find him grabbing Rally’s or Portillo’s — keeping it simple after delivering something unforgettable.

What’s next

RAB3L is currently working on new mixes and releases, steadily building his catalogue while pushing toward bigger opportunities. His goals are clear: more shows, touring, and eventually landing on major platforms like Insomniac events, as well as international stages in Ibiza and the UK.

But beyond the milestones, there’s a deeper drive behind it all. He’s not just chasing success — he’s building proof. Proof that consistency pays off. Proof that starting young and staying committed matters. Proof that dreams, when backed by action, turn real.

And he wants the people who’ve supported him from day one to be part of that journey all the way through.

Why book RAB3L

If you’re a promoter, collective, or venue looking to bring in an artist who delivers both technically and energetically, RAB3L checks every box:

  • Deep genre versatility across bass house, tech house, UKG, and bassline
  • Proven performance experience in respected LA venues
  • Strong crowd-reading ability across all audience types
  • Professional preparation and technical knowledge
  • High-energy sets that translate from clubs to festival environments

This is an artist who doesn’t just show up. He elevates the room.

The quick version

RAB3L isn’t waiting for a moment. He’s creating it — set by set, crowd by crowd. And if you’re paying attention right now, you’re catching him on the rise.
