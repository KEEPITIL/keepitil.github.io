DJ SUSAN

The Tech House Connector

San Diego, CA
Tech House · House
KEEPITIL Artist

DJ Susan — the alias of San Diego producer Steven Glathe — has spent the last few years turning gritty, high-energy tech house into a full-on community project. Self-described as the "Tech House Tony Robbins," his mission is simple: get people to connect on the dancefloor. Big builds, heavy drops, and rolling basslines have made him a Miami Music Week breakout and a Beatport chart regular, while the label he founded, Hood Politics Records, has grown into a real platform for underground producers worldwide. KEEPITIL features DJ Susan as one of San Diego's most visible underground house exports.

"Tech House Tony Robbins" — big builds, heavy drops, gritty basslines, and one mission: connect the room.
THE SOUND
High-energy tech house
DJ Susan's sound is a potent blend of big builds, heavy drops, and gritty basslines — tech house engineered for maximum dancefloor reaction. It's playful and physical at once, the kind of set that turns a club into a single moving crowd.
Connection over polish
More than chart positions, DJ Susan talks about the dancefloor as a place to connect. That ethos — getting strangers locked into the same groove — is the throughline from his earliest San Diego sets to his biggest festival rooms.
THE RESUME
Dirtybird debut
In 2026 DJ Susan made his label debut on Dirtybird with the single "Transformation," joining one of dance music's most recognizable independent imprints. It followed earlier releases on Glasgow Underground, Repopulate Mars, and Helix Records.
Hood Politics Records
DJ Susan founded and runs Hood Politics Records, a label that has become a beacon for underground tech house and a launchpad for artists around the world — extending his influence well past his own DJ sets.
Chart-topping cuts
Tracks like "TALKIN" and "Secrets" hit number one in Beatport's bass house and tech house hype charts, and his Miami Music Week 2022 run helped cement him as a name to watch in the genre.
THE SCENE
San Diego on the map
DJ Susan is part of a wave of San Diego artists pushing the city's underground house and tech house identity onto national stages. KEEPITIL features him as part of documenting how Southern California's dance scene reaches well beyond LA and Orange County.
