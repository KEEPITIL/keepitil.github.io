KEEPITIL June 2026 Picks — The Underground Events That Mattered

📅 June 23, 2026 ✍ KEEPITIL Editorial 🎵 OC & LA Underground

June 2026 delivered across the board for the SoCal underground. From the massive to the intimate, the month proved that the scene is operating at a high level on both sides of the production equation — organizers investing in quality, audiences showing up and engaging.

The Picks That Delivered

Summer of the Living Dead at Queen Mary Waterfront set the tone early — the Bassrush-curated lineup hit exactly right, Pendulum and Hybrid Minds both delivered headline-worthy performances, and the waterfront setting added a dimension that enclosed venues simply cannot replicate.

June showed what the SoCal scene is capable of when organizers commit to quality over quantity.

The Warehouse Standouts

Several underground warehouse events across LA’s Arts District and OC’s industrial zones provided the intimate counterpoint to the large-format events. The most memorable combined serious sound system investment with lineups that demonstrated genuine curatorial taste.

What’s Coming in July

July picks up immediately with Lost in Dreams Festival (July 11-12), Factory 93 at Exchange LA (July 11), and a strong calendar of club nights through the rest of the month. Check the Events page for the full KEEPITIL July calendar.
