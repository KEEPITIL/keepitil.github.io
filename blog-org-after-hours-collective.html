ABOUT AFTER HOURS COLLECTIVE

After Hours Collective is a Los Angeles event organization that specifically produces events designed for late-night and into-the-morning schedules — programming that starts at midnight and runs until daylight, serving the dedicated underground community that exists specifically beyond LA's 2AM last-call cutoff.

By operating in non-licensed spaces and time frames that conventional clubs can't service, After Hours Collective has created a specific niche in the LA underground for events that honor the tradition of after-hours culture — smaller, more intimate, more musically focused than the events that happen in the licensed hours.

Q&A
After-hours events operate in legally ambiguous territory — how do you navigate that?

With care, community trust, and a commitment to safety above all else. The underground has a long history of after-hours culture, and that history includes both the best experiences electronic music has to offer and real risks that need to be managed responsibly. We take the safety and community trust dimensions as seriously as we take the music.

Who comes to after-hours events versus main-night events?

People who are specifically there for the music rather than the social experience of being seen at an event. After 2AM, the casual attendees have gone home. What's left is the dedicated community that actually loves the music and is willing to rearrange their schedules to experience it at its best. Those are the audiences that make underground events worth running.
