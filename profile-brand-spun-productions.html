About Spun Productions

Spun Productions is a San Francisco-based underground electronic music promotion company with roots deep in the Bay Area rave scene. Producing events across house, techno, and experimental electronic genres, Spun has been a consistent force in SF's underground community for years.

The team specializes in intimate, community-focused events that honor the Bay Area's rich history as a pioneer in underground electronic music. From warehouse parties to club nights, Spun brings quality curation and production to every event.

What They Offer

Underground electronic event production, house and techno event promotion, Bay Area artist support, and community-focused events throughout San Francisco.
