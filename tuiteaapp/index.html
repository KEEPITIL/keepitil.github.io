TUITEA

A private app for one family.

TUITEA keeps what a family actually wants to keep — who everyone is and how they are related, the photographs and the stories behind them, the health and the everyday running of a household — in one place that belongs to you and is shown to nobody else.

Join the beta

How it works

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store — Apple's app for trying beta software.
  2. Open the invitation link above on your iPhone and accept it.
  3. Install TUITEA from TestFlight and open it.
  4. If someone has invited you to their family, open their invitation link and ask to join. Nothing about that family is visible to you until somebody in it approves you.
DeviceiPhone

This is a beta. Things will be rough in places, and we would rather hear about it than not. Use Report a problem inside the app — it goes straight to us with the screen you were on, and it is the fastest way to get something fixed.

Your family's records stay yours. A household's information is visible only to people in that household who have been approved. Health sharing is each person's own choice, and nobody else — not even the person who set the household up — can see or change it.

TUITEA is part of KEEPITIL.
