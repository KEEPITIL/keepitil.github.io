4 Referral Programs That Fund the Underground (July 2026)

KEEPITILJuly 2, 2026OC / LA

KEEPITIL doesn't run on venture money or a paywall — it runs on the scene. The event calendar, the artist profiles, the culture blog: all free, and we want to keep it that way. These four referral programs are how the lights stay on. Every link below pays KEEPITIL something, so we're listing exactly what each one pays, who it's actually for, and where it falls short. How we picked these (and why we show the payouts)

There are hundreds of referral programs on the internet and most of them are noise. The four below made the cut for one reason: they map to people who are already in this community — artists releasing music, ravers buying gear, organizers throwing shows, and broke creatives trying to scrape together ticket money. If a program didn't fit one of those four people, it didn't make the list. And we publish the payout on every offer because the underground runs on trust; if we're earning off your click, you deserve to know how much.

1. DistroKid — for artists releasing music

If you're a producer or DJ sitting on finished tracks, DistroKid is the standard move: unlimited uploads to Spotify, Apple Music, TikTok, and the rest of the major stores for one flat annual fee, and you keep 100% of your royalties.[1] Most of the SoCal artists on our directory already distribute through it. Signing up through our link gets you 7% off your first year — and pays KEEPITIL $10 per referral.

Real talkThe flat fee only makes sense if you're actually releasing. If you drop one track a year, a pay-per-release distributor might be cheaper. If you're releasing quarterly or more, DistroKid wins on math.

3. FreeCash — ticket money for doing tasks on your phone

FreeCash pays you to test apps and games and answer surveys, with cash-outs to PayPal, crypto, or gift cards.[3] It's not a job and it won't replace one — but knocking out offers during a slow week can genuinely cover a ticket to Friday's show. Signing up through our link pays KEEPITIL up to $12.50 per referral.

4. Posh — for organizers listing their next event

If you're throwing a show, Posh is the ticketing platform we see the most across the OC/LA underground — clean event pages, built-in text-message marketing to your past attendees, and payouts organizers don't have to chase.[4] Creating your organizer group through our link supports KEEPITIL on qualifying signups, and costs you nothing.
