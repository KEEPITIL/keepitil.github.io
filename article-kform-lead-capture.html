KFORM: Fast Lead Capture Wired Straight Into KEEPITIL

A form and lead-capture builder for events, RSVPs and creator sign-ups — every submission flowing straight into the KEEPITIL ecosystem instead of a spreadsheet you'll never open again.

KEEPITIL

Every promoter has the same graveyard: a folder of half-finished Google Forms, a screenshot of DMs, a notes app full of "text me to RSVP." The interest was real. The system to catch it never was. KFORM is the system.

KFORM is a coming-soon marketing app from KEEPITIL — a fast form and lead-capture builder made for the specific things this scene actually collects: event RSVPs, guest lists, creator sign-ups, brand inquiries, mailing lists. The difference from every generic form tool isn't the fields. It's where the answers go. KFORM is wired straight into KEEPITIL, so a submission isn't a dead row in a spreadsheet — it's a lead that lands inside the same ecosystem where your profile, your events, and your audience already live.

What it's built to do

The goal is speed and connection. Building a capture form should take a minute, not an afternoon of wrestling a bloated SaaS tool built for enterprise HR departments. KFORM is meant to spin up the form you need — an RSVP for Friday, a sign-up for a new artist call, a lead form under a brand partnership — and drop it somewhere people can actually reach it: a link in a bio, a QR code at the door, a block on your KEEPITIL profile.

Then the part that matters: the responses flow into KEEPITIL. Instead of exporting a CSV and re-importing it somewhere, the leads you capture become usable inside the platform — feeding your event, your audience, your follow-up. It closes the loop between "someone was interested" and "I can actually do something with that," which is exactly the gap that kills most grassroots marketing.

Who it's for

KFORM is built for the people doing the organizing. If you're an event organizer, it's your RSVP and guest-list intake without paying a ticketing platform for a form. If you're a creator or artist, it's how you capture the people who want in — mailing list, collab requests, early-access sign-ups — and keep them somewhere that actually connects to your work. If you're a brand running a promotion or a partnership, it's a clean inquiry pipe that doesn't lose leads in a shared inbox.

It's for anyone who's ever watched genuine interest evaporate because there was no simple way to catch it in the moment. The whole design premise is that the moment of interest is fragile, and the tool that captures it has to be faster than the moment is short.

Why KEEPITIL is building it

KFORM is a natural extension of what KEEPITIL already is: infrastructure for the creative economy. The platform's job is to help underground creatives get discovered and build something durable — and capturing an audience is a core part of that. A discovery platform that can send people to your profile but can't help you catch the ones who want to go deeper is only doing half the job. KFORM is the other half.

It's also part of KEEPITIL's ecosystem philosophy — nothing built in isolation. KFORM leans on the same backbone as the events tools, the profiles, and the radio, so the leads you capture aren't stranded in a standalone app; they're part of the connected graph that makes the whole platform more useful the more you use it.
