Sound System Guide — What to Listen For at Underground Events

📅 June 12, 2026 ✍ KEEPITIL Editorial 🎵 OC & LA Underground

Sound system quality is the most underappreciated variable in the underground event experience. Most attendees can feel the difference between a great and a poor system without being able to articulate why. Here’s what to listen for.

The Sub Bass Test

Crouch down. The sub bass should feel more physical lower in the room. If the bass is only in your chest when you’re standing, the subs are placed and tuned for mid-field rather than near-field — you’re not getting full system performance. The best systems fill the floor with sub bass from the ground up.

A great sound system doesn’t make you think about the sound. It disappears and you just hear music.

Mid-Range Clarity

Mid-range clarity is what distinguishes premium PA from adequate PA. Can you hear the hi-hats clearly over the kick? Does the snare have snap? Can you follow the lead melodic element through a dense arrangement? Poor mid-range causes everything above 200Hz to smear together into noise.

Room Acoustics

A great sound system can be destroyed by a bad room. Concrete surfaces without treatment cause reflections that muddy everything. Listen for echo patterns and standing waves — they will tell you whether the organizer invested in acoustic treatment.

What Great Systems Sound Like

Funktion-One, d&b audiotechnik, and L-Acoustics are the benchmarks. In a well-tuned room on any of these systems, you can listen at high SPL for hours without fatigue. The sound is clear, spatial, and physically present without being painful.
