KODA

Deep Tech and Minimal From San Diego's Underground

📍 San Diego, CA 🎵 Deep Tech / Minimal Techno / Tech House ⭐ KEEPITIL Artist

KODA operates at the intersection of deep tech, minimal, and the slow-build techno that rewards patience. Based in San Diego, they've built a devoted audience through consistent underground bookings and a sound that prioritizes texture and tension over immediate impact — music designed for the long haul of a four-hour set.

"Minimal music is maximally demanding of its audience — and that's exactly the point."
THE STORY
San Diego's underground electronic scene is largely invisible to outsiders — what's actually happening there?
A lot. SD has been building quietly for a long time. The Tijuana connection is real and important — there's a cross-border electronic music culture that informs what happens on the SD side in ways that the LA scene simply doesn't have access to. Events run later, the music tends toward the deeper, slower end of the spectrum, and there's less concern with fitting into whatever is happening in the broader industry.
Where did the minimal techno obsession come from?
Hearing Ricardo Villalobos on a recording and genuinely not understanding what was happening — the music was doing something to the room that I couldn't explain by analyzing the parts. I spent about six months just listening to that record trying to figure out how it worked. That process of obsessive listening is what sent me down the rabbit hole of production.
THE CRAFT
How do you structure a minimal set that keeps people engaged over four hours?
You're not trying to keep people engaged in the way a pop concert tries to keep people engaged. You're creating a space they inhabit. The set evolves slowly enough that any single moment feels static, but the cumulative effect over hours is a complete journey. It requires the audience to surrender their need for constant stimulation, which is its own kind of art.
What's your relationship with the OC underground, and how do SD and OC scenes connect?
There's real cross-pollination — artists who play in OC regularly end up playing SD, and vice versa. The two scenes have different characters but they share a commitment to the music over the spectacle that you don't always find in LA proper. KEEPITIL being a bridge between those geographies is genuinely valuable for that reason.
