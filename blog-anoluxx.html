★ KEEPITIL PRESENTS

ANOLUXX

One City At A Time

Los Angeles, CA
Hard Techno
KEEPITIL Artist

ANOLUXX plays hard techno the way it was meant to live — loud, fast, and built for a warehouse with the lights off. Since launching the project in November 2021, ANOLUXX has chased the underground one city at a time, turning a string of shows and festival slots into a name that now headlines big-room hard techno nights across the Los Angeles circuit. KEEPITIL features ANOLUXX as part of documenting the new generation pushing California's hard techno scene.

Big-room hard techno with no apologies — kick-forward, relentless, and made for the moment a dark room locks into a single tempo.
THE SOUND
Big-room hard techno
ANOLUXX works in the harder, faster end of techno — the kick-forward, high-BPM sound that has surged through warehouse and DIY rooms over the last few years. The sets are built for scale: pounding low end, peak-time energy, and the kind of momentum that keeps a floor moving from the first track to the last.
Built for the warehouse
This is music made for the room it plays in. ANOLUXX leans into the physical impact of hard techno — pressure, repetition, release — rather than chasing radio polish. It is a sound designed to be felt as much as heard, which is exactly why it translates to late-night, no-frills underground spaces.
THE RESUME
Started in 2021
ANOLUXX launched the project in November 2021 and has been building steadily ever since — taking the underground "one city at a time" through a run of shows and festival appearances rather than waiting for a single breakout moment.
Headlining LA rooms
ANOLUXX has moved into headline territory on the Los Angeles hard techno circuit, including big-room nights at 1720 — one of the city's go-to warehouse spaces for harder electronic music. It is the kind of room that rewards exactly the sound ANOLUXX builds toward.
Find the music
ANOLUXX keeps an active catalog of mixes and originals on SoundCloud, and documents shows and releases on Instagram — the clearest window into where the project is heading next.
THE SCENE
Part of LA's hard techno surge
ANOLUXX sits inside the wave of Southern California artists driving harder, faster techno through the region's warehouse and DIY circuit — a scene that has bounced back stronger than ever and increasingly draws crowds chasing the European-style hard techno sound on home turf. KEEPITIL features ANOLUXX as part of documenting that moment in California's underground.
