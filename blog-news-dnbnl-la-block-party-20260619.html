B&L Shuts Down a Block: DNBNL LA Hits Mateo Warehouse Sept 12

KEEPITIL Desk June 19, 2026 DTLA Arts District

Brownies & Lemonade is taking its drum & bass series out of the club and into the street. The LA institution just announced the first-ever DNBNL LA Block Party — a two-stage, day-to-night DnB rave at Mateo Warehouse in the Arts District of Downtown LA on Saturday, September 12. They're shutting the block down. Indoor, outdoor, sundown to last call. This is the biggest swing the DNBNL banner has taken since it started.

For the uninitiated: DNBNL is the drum & bass arm of Brownies & Lemonade, the homegrown LA crew that's spent the better part of a decade turning all-ages bass shows into a citywide tastemaker brand. The series turns five this year, and rather than mark it with another room at the Roxy or a one-stage warehouse night, B&L is going for the full block-party format — two stages, an immersive indoor/outdoor build, and a footprint that spills past the venue doors and into the Arts District itself.

"A homegrown LA crew shutting down a block for 170+ BPM music is exactly the kind of statement DnB hasn't gotten to make on the West Coast in years."

Why It Matters For The Underground

Drum & bass has always run hot in SoCal's after-hours rooms — it just rarely gets the headline treatment that house and techno pull at the festival level. A B&L block party flips that script. When the crew that broke half of LA's bass talent decides DnB deserves its own street takeover, it tells every local promoter and bedroom producer chasing 170 that there's real ceiling here. The genre isn't a niche slot anymore; it's worth shutting down a block for.

No lineup yet — B&L is only promising "a world-class lineup of DNB heavy hitters and favs," with names to come. But the format alone is the news. The Arts District has been quietly absorbing warehouse culture for years, and a permitted, two-stage daytime-into-night rave in that corridor signals the scene is maturing without losing the grime. For the OC and LA artists grinding the same circuit, more daylight on DnB means more rooms, more bookings, more oxygen.
