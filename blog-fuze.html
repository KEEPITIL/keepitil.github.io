★ KEEPITIL PRESENTS

FUZE

Dubstep and Bass Engineering from the IE Underground

📍 Riverside, CA 🎵 Dubstep / Bass / Hybrid Trap ⭐ KEEPITIL Artist

FUZE is Riverside's answer to the question of what dubstep sounds like when it's built by someone who studies physics — sets of enormous precision and impact, rooted in the Inland Empire warehouse circuit and increasingly reaching into OC and LA venues. The IE underground produced FUZE; the broader SoCal scene is starting to take notice.

"Sound at that level isn't heard — it's felt. My job is to engineer that feeling."
THE STORY
Riverside isn't a city most people associate with electronic music. What is the culture like there?
Riverside has a massive music community that mainstream coverage completely ignores. The UC Riverside student population brings in a lot of people who are genuinely passionate about music from all over, and the city has enough industrial space and tolerant geography to support the kind of warehouse events that aren't possible in denser cities. The scene is real, it's committed, and it doesn't need outside validation to exist.
Your production approach has been described as 'engineered' — what does that mean in practice?
I approach bass sound design like an acoustics problem. The difference between a dubstep bass that hits and one that doesn't is physics — frequency content, transient behavior, harmonic structure, how the sound interacts with a room at volume. I spend a lot of time studying why certain bass sounds work at a physical level and reverse engineering that into my production. The goal is basses that feel like they bypass your hearing and go directly to your nervous system.
THE CRAFT
How does the IE scene's character influence the music you make?
The IE doesn't do things halfway. When you go to a warehouse event in San Bernardino or Riverside, the crowd came specifically for the heaviest possible music experience — they're not there to be gently introduced to anything. That audience shapes how I produce. I'm making music for rooms where the subwoofer fills the whole space and the crowd is prepared for maximum impact.
What does your trajectory look like beyond Riverside in 2026?
Building relationships in OC through KEEPITIL and some direct connections with promoters, and working on original tracks that I want to release this year. The IE underground has built the foundation; now it's time to extend.
