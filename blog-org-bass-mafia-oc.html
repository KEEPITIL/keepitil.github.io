About Bass Mafia OC

Bass Mafia OC is Anaheim's premier bass music collective, bringing heavy dubstep, riddim, and bass-driven sounds to Orange County's underground scene. Known for jaw-dropping sound systems and headbanging lineups, Bass Mafia events are a staple for OC's bass heads.

The Bass Mafia crew is more than an event company — they're a community of bass music obsessives dedicated to supporting local producers and connecting OC fans with touring heavy-hitters.

What They Do

Bass music events, dubstep and riddim shows, sound system curation, and local bass artist development in Orange County.

Upcoming Events

Bass Mafia OC hosts regular shows in Anaheim and LA. Follow @bassmafia for upcoming lineup drops and ticket info.

Q & A
What kind of music does Bass Mafia play?
Dubstep, riddim, heavy bass, neurofunk, and all flavors of bass-forward underground electronic music.
How do I get on the Bass Mafia lineup?
Bass producers and DJs can reach out via Instagram DM. They actively support local artists.
