KEEPITIL RADIO

HOW YOU EARN

Submit your work. KEEPITIL puts it in front of people through radio, playlists, articles and features. What the platform earns from partners and royalties funds creator rewards — and more creators bring more audience.

RADIO

What you submit
Your music, with a link to the track.
How it works
Accepted tracks enter KEEPITIL Radio rotation. There are two levels: standard rotation, and a royalty-partner tier with more scheduled placement.
What you give
Standard rotation asks nothing. The royalty tier is a participation arrangement — a share of the track's royalties through your existing distribution, agreed in writing.
What you receive
Guaranteed monthly airtime on KEEPITIL Radio. Royalty partners also receive recurring scheduled rotation, genre playlist placement, chart and leaderboard participation, editorial and review opportunities, artist highlights and promotional assets — for as long as the arrangement is active.

PLAYLISTS

What you submit
One curated SoundCloud playlist, with a portrait (2:3) cover.
How it works
SoundCloud stays the source. Edit the playlist there and KEEPITIL plays the change — you never resubmit tracks.
What you give
Nothing. One playlist per person keeps the wall curated.
What you receive
Your playlist listed on KEEPITIL and inside the radio playlist ecosystem, where listeners can find and play it. Rotation is offered where scheduling allows; royalty-partner placement comes first.

CREATE

What you submit
Creative work entered into a KEEPITIL competition.
How it works
Every valid entry gets exposure — you do not have to win first.
What you give
Your entry, and accurate credit for who made it.
What you receive
Placement in Culture, an editorial article built around your work, and discovery through the feed. Winners receive the residency package: continued coverage, featured placement, a winner badge, a permanent record, and participation in the creator reward programme for the residency period.

PARTNERS

What you submit
Nothing to submit — you take part by referring and sharing.
How it works
Two separate things, deliberately not mixed. You may earn directly from an eligible referral or affiliate programme. Separately, KEEPITIL puts a share of what it earns from partners into the creator reward pool.
What you give
Nothing beyond taking part.
What you receive
Your own referral earnings where a programme applies, and — as a resident or winner — a share of the creator reward pool for your period.

KEEPITIL guarantees what KEEPITIL controls: placement, rotation, articles and features. How many people listen, watch or follow is down to the audience, and nobody honest promises that.
