How to use it (so it actually works)

Apply to clean skin before heavier creams, AM and/or PM. Niacinamide layers well with almost everything (including vitamin C, despite the old myth). Give it 8–12 weeks for pore/oil changes. Always follow with SPF in the morning.

FAQ

Is a higher % better? No — 5–10% is the sweet spot for most people; above ~12% raises irritation risk without much extra benefit.
