SWERVE

Controlled Chaos, Authentic Connection

🎵 Listen to SWERVE
SWERVE · SWERVE'$ SUMMER HEATER$

Servando Portillo put on his first pair of headphones at thirteen and heard Nightcrawlers — "Push The Feeling On" — and felt something he can still describe today: escape. That was the beginning. By fifteen, he was playing a 21+ venue illegally. By sixteen, the Alexandria Hotel. SWERVE has been building his library — and his brand — ever since, across decades and genres from vinyl to USB, from hip-hop MC to Tech House, from battle dancer to peak-hour DJ.

"From the moment I put on my headphones and listened to Nightcrawlers — Push The Feeling On — I was able to escape my reality."
THE STORY
When did you start DJing?
Age 13 on vinyl, listening to Power 106. Absorbing everything. First gig was The Arena in LA at 15 — illegally, 21+ venue. Then Alexandria Hotel, Circus Circus. The grind started young.
What moment made you realize this was it?
The first time I put on headphones and heard Nightcrawlers — Push The Feeling On. I was able to escape my reality. That feeling never left. I've been chasing it and creating it for other people ever since.
Who influenced your sound?
Alexander AM-FM, Bad Boy Bill, DJ Tragic, Richard Humpty Vision. Legends who understood that DJing is about connection, not just selection. That lineage is in my DNA.
THE CRAFT
What makes your sets different?
Process, curation, beautiful transitions, alternative selection — the time and effort put in before I ever step into the booth. What you hear in a SWERVE set is the result of years of deliberate work on the back end.
What energy do you bring?
Controlled chaos. I build up anticipation, then drop the right track at the right moment. Nothing is random. Every peak was planned. Every release was earned.
What's your secret weapon with diverse crowds?
My music library. I'm definitely older — and that means I can play for an 18-year-old or a 70-year-old. The depth of my catalog is my biggest weapon. Age, genre, mood — I'll find the track that connects.
What can someone expect when they book you?
Professionalism, reliability, and results. I show up ready. No surprises on the set-up, no missing equipment, no late arrivals. I treat every booking like a mission.
"I am here to make people have an authentic emotional connection and escape from reality."
THE HIGHLIGHTS
Dream venue?
Red Rock Canyon, Mirage NYC, Echo Stage. Venues where the room matches the ambition of the set.
Track that always gets the crowd going?
Dom Dolla — Girls. Works every time. No exceptions.
What are you working on?
New Minimal Deep Tech set. Music production and remixes. Pushing the brand. Trying to headline at Bleu. The next chapter is already in motion.
OFF THE DECKS
Something people wouldn't expect?
I used to be a battle dancer and hip-hop MC and writer. So the performance element, the reading of an audience, the instinct for when to hit — all of that comes from way before the DJ booth.
Pre-set ritual?
Check USB sticks, go over transitions, check the guest list, push social media, rally up with friends. Then walk out like you own the room.
Late-night food?
Tacos. Quesadillas as a close second. Always.
Outside music?
Gym life. Dog nature walks. Keep the body and mind sharp so the sets stay sharp.
