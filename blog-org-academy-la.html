About Academy LA

Academy LA is a multi-venue nightclub complex in Los Angeles featuring three distinct rooms — the Academy (main room), the 1720 warehouse space, and the intimate 188 venue. Together they create one of LA's most versatile underground event experiences.

Academy LA is known for hosting a wide range of underground events from techno to bass music, while also featuring intimate DJ sets and live performances. The 1720 warehouse space, in particular, has become one of LA's most beloved underground event venues.

What They Do

Multi-room nightclub events, underground electronic music programming, warehouse events (1720), and intimate club experiences in Los Angeles.

Upcoming Events

Academy LA hosts events Friday and Saturday. 1720 hosts special events and underground nights throughout the week. Visit academyla.com for the calendar.

Q & A
What's the difference between Academy, 1720, and 188?
Academy is the main club room, 1720 is the adjacent warehouse space for underground events, and 188 is the intimate venue for smaller shows.
