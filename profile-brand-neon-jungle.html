Neon Jungle

Artist Profile · Santa Ana

Neon Jungle is a Santa Ana-based maker and retailer of festival accessories, handmade wearables, and event goods created specifically for California's electronic music community. Founded by a collective of artists and crafters who met at OC underground events, Neon Jungle operates at the intersection of handmade culture and underground aesthetics.

Santa Ana
OC & LA

The Story

Everything sold by Neon Jungle is made by hand in small batches or sourced directly from independent makers. No mass production, no fast fashion manufacturing, no compromise on material quality. The emphasis is on accessories that last through multiple festival seasons and age with the community that wears them.

Because the underground community deserves better than the same factory-made stuff you find at every vendor booth at every festival. Handmade means unique, means quality, means the person who made it genuinely cared. When you buy something from Neon Jungle you're buying something that a specific human being made with their hands.

We table at events, we're part of the community ourselves, and we build relationships with organizers and artists who reflect the values we care about. KEEPITIL's network has been helpful for connecting with events and communities we weren't already reaching.
