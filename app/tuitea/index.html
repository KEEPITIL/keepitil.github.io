This is a beta. Things will be rough in places, and we would rather hear about it than not. Use Report a problem inside the app — it reaches us with the screen you were on, and it is the fastest way to get something fixed.

Your family's records stay yours. A household is visible only to people in it who have been approved. Health sharing is each person's own choice, and nobody else — not even whoever set the household up — can see or change it.
