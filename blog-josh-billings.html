JOSH BILLINGS

Two Decades of West Coast House

Newport Beach, CA
House / Tech House / Deep House
KEEPITIL Artist

Few names carry more weight in Orange County house music than Josh Billings. Active in the West Coast house scene since 2000, he is the creator and founder of Focus — the long-running weekly that has kept house music alive in Newport Beach since 2003. As a DJ, producer and promoter, he has spent more than two decades building the kind of dependable local infrastructure that lets an underground scene survive between the big touring nights.

Since 2003, Focus has been Orange County's house music constant — and Josh Billings has been the name behind it.
THE WEEKLY
Focus, since 2003
Josh Billings is the founder of Focus, a house music event he launched in Newport Beach in 2003. More than twenty years on, it remains one of Orange County's most consistent homes for quality house — a weekly that has outlasted countless venues and trends and still anchors the county's dance floors.
THE RESUME
Two decades on the West Coast
Since 2000, Billings has been a working part of the West Coast house movement as a DJ, producer and promoter. Over those years he has shared the decks with a who's-who of house music — names like Kaskade, Fedde Le Grand, Mark Farina, James Zabiela and Nic Fanciulli among them — while keeping his focus rooted in the local Orange County scene.
In the studio
More recently Billings has leaned into his own productions and remixes, releasing original work alongside his DJ schedule. His catalog and mixes live on SoundCloud, and he continues to put out new material under his own name.
THE SCENE
An OC institution
Josh Billings represents the kind of long-haul figure every healthy scene depends on: the promoter and resident who keeps a room open week after week so newer artists have somewhere to play. KEEPITIL features him as part of documenting the people who built — and keep building — Orange County's underground house culture.
