Underground Techno Picks — June 2026 Essential Tracks

📅 June 22, 2026 ✍ KEEPITIL Editorial 🎵 OC & LA Underground

June 2026 delivered a strong batch of underground techno from the SoCal scene and beyond. These are the tracks that defined the month — the ones that moved floors at Exchange LA, at warehouse nights in the Arts District, and at the intimate club nights happening across OC and LA every weekend.

Picks from OC and LA Producers

The local scene produced standout material this month. Hard techno with genuine industrial muscle, deep mechanical house that works late in the set, and a handful of tracks that bridge the gap between dancefloor function and genuine musical intelligence.

The best techno doesn’t make you think about it — it just makes you move. Then later, you think about why.

International Picks Relevant to the SoCal Scene

Several international releases landed this month that have been appearing in sets across LA and OC. These are the tracks being played by artists with genuine underground credibility, not the ones dominating the algorithmic playlists.

How to Find Underground Music

Beyond RA and Bandcamp, the best way to find underground techno is to follow the local DJs whose taste you trust — Demek, NOCTUA, CLAWZ, and others on the KEEPITIL roster are sharing and releasing music regularly. Follow them, support their Bandcamp pages, and show up to their events.
