FAQ

QUESTIONS


What kinds of events do you accept? +
OC and LA County underground electronic music events only — techno, house, bass, D&B, trance. Quality over quantity.
How does the 20% cut work? +
The 20% is taken from ticket revenue processed through your Eventbrite or Posh account. This covers all four marketing channels — no additional cost.
How long does approval take? +
We aim to review within 48 hours. Once approved, your event is added to the Explore page immediately.
Do I need a Posh or Eventbrite account? +
Yes — you'll need at least one active ticket link for your event to be approved. Free events are accepted too.
Can I submit multiple events? +
Yes. Once your organizer account is approved, you can submit as many events as you like.
