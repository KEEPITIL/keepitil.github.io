LVNKY BONEZ

The Bass Doesn't Lie

Los Angeles, CA
Dubstep · DnB · Bass House
KEEPITIL Artist
🎵 Listen to LVNKY BONEZ
LVNKY BONEZ · Captain Crunch (free DL)

Brian West didn't come up through DJ school or industry connections. He came up through raw obsession — the kind that starts when you make your first track, post it online, and suddenly realize that the world heard something. Lvnky Bonez has been building his underground Dubstep and DnB catalog since 2022, and what started as a side project has become a heat source you can't ignore.

"I engage the crowd and create relationships as I'm playing."
THE STORY
When did you start DJing?
Started in 2022. Not that long ago on paper, but you can't measure growth by years — you measure it by output. And the output has been relentless.
What was your first gig like?
Better than I expected. That's always the best kind of first experience — when the crowd gives you more than you thought they would. It sets the tone for everything after.
What moment made you realize this is what you wanted to do?
When I made my first song and posted it. That was the moment. Seeing people respond to something you built from nothing — that's a feeling you can't walk away from.
Who influenced your sound?
Knife Party, Deadmau5, Daft Punk, Excision. The architects. Artists who didn't just make music — they built worlds out of sound. That's the standard.
THE CRAFT
What makes your sets different?
I engage the crowd and create relationships as I'm playing. It's not just pressing play — it's reading the room, feeling the energy shift, and responding to it in real time. You're not watching a playlist, you're watching a conversation.
What energy do you bring?
Hype, hype, and more hype. There is no version of a Lvnky Bonez set where the energy is low. That's not what we're here for.
What crowd do you play best for?
Underground. People who know what they're there for. Bass heads. The ones who came because they actually listen to Dubstep and DnB — not just because it's a party.
What experience should someone expect when they book you?
Dancing and the heavy-hitting vibes. If you came to stand still, you booked the wrong DJ. If you came to feel it in your chest, let's talk.
"I'm creating a lot of heat on SoundCloud and it's taking off. Pay attention."
THE HIGHLIGHTS
Favorite set you've played?
Birthday party for a friend's daughter — they loved the bass. The ones who don't know they're bass heads until you introduce them to it? That's the best crowd to play for.
Dream venue?
Exchange LA. Apocalypse. The underground spots built for exactly this kind of sound.
One track that always gets the crowd going?
Captain Crunch — my track. Nothing hits the same as watching people respond to something you made.
OFF THE DECKS
Something people wouldn't expect to know about you?
I like binaural beats. Outside of music I spin fire and dance. So yeah — there's more to Lvnky Bonez than just the DJ booth.
Pre-set ritual?
Connecting with close friends. Ground yourself before you go out there. That energy you carry into the booth carries into the room.
Late-night food after a set?
Taco Bell. Classic. No debate.
Day or night?
Night. That's when bass sounds the way it's supposed to sound.
What's next?
EP in the works. ATLriddim show in July. More production, more releases — the heat keeps building. Watch SoundCloud.
