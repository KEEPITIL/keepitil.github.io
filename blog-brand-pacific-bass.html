ABOUT PACIFIC BASS

Pacific Bass is a Los Angeles-based audio equipment supplier focused specifically on the needs of underground DJs and producers — not the professional broadcast or live sound market, but the specific requirements of club DJs, bedroom producers, and warehouse event sound systems.

From CDJ accessories and DJ mixers to studio monitors and studio treatment, Pacific Bass curates gear selections based on actual use in underground performance and production environments. Staff members are active DJs and producers, not salespeople — every recommendation comes from direct experience.

WHAT THEY OFFER
CDJ and controller accessories
DJ mixers and crossfaders
Studio monitors and headphones
Portable sound systems for events
Production hardware and synthesizers
Cables, adapters, and accessories

Q&A
What makes Pacific Bass different from Guitar Center or Sweetwater?

Specialization and expertise. Guitar Center sells everything to everyone. We sell specific things to specific people — DJs and producers who need accurate advice about gear that performs in actual underground conditions. We stock things those stores don't carry because those stores don't understand the underground market.

Do you support local artists and events beyond just selling gear?

Yes — we loan equipment for events we believe in, sponsor shows from underground promoters we respect, and occasionally donate gear to developing artists who need it to grow. The underground scene is our community, not just our market.
