RAB3L

Built From The Booth, Raised By Sound

Torrance, CA
Bass House · Tech House · UKG · Bassline
KEEPITIL Artist
🎵 Listen to RAB3L
RAB3L · RAB3L UK Garage Mix

Some DJs grow into the craft. Others are born into it. RAB3L is the latter. Hailing from Torrance, California, Bryan Ricardo Mentado has been immersed in DJ culture since the age of eight — inspired by a family steeped in music and a natural ability to read a room that most artists spend years trying to develop.

"Music speaks in different ways and I'm really good at reading music and people."
THE STORY
When did you start DJing and what inspired you?
Started DJing since I was 8 years old. What inspired me was the family business — music was always in the house, always in the culture. It wasn't a choice, it was just what we did.
What was your first real gig like?
Eight years old. That was the first real gig. No nerves — just sound and crowd. That's when it started to feel real.
What moment made you realize this is what you wanted to do?
Music speaks in different ways and I'm really good at reading music and people. That realization hit early. Not everyone can do both.
Who influenced your sound the most growing up?
My dad was the foundation. Then Tchami and AC Slater — they showed me what Bass House and Tech House could actually feel like at full power.
THE CRAFT
What makes your sets different from other DJs?
My knowledge throughout the years of gigs I've had. Years in the booth builds something you can't fake — instinct, selection, timing. That's what separates me.
What kind of energy do you bring to a room?
Bouncy, festival, euphoric, groovy. Whatever the room needs, I can find it. That's the skill — reading 500 people and giving them what they didn't know they were there for.
What type of crowd do you perform best for?
All types — because of my knowledge base. I've played enough shows to know how to move any crowd. Age, genre preference, mood — it doesn't matter. I'll get there.
If someone books you, what experience should they expect?
The years of knowledge. Always prepared. I don't wing it — I study, I curate, I come ready for whatever direction the night takes.
THE HIGHLIGHTS
What's been your favorite set you've ever played?
Avalon Hollywood and Academy LA. Those rooms hit different. When you're playing for a crowd that truly gets it, everything clicks into place.
What's your dream venue or festival?
Utopia. AfterMidnight.LA events. All events connected to Insomniac. Even some events in Ibiza and the UK — that's where the sound goes global.
One track that always gets the crowd going?
Notion - The Days. Every single time.
"I'm going to the top with my dreams and trying to show people that they are becoming reality — and those who have been with me since day one will still be with me till the end."
OFF THE DECKS
Something people don't know about you?
I'm pretty knowledgeable on DJ equipment. Outside of music I'm a tech for Chevy — so I understand both machines: the car engine and the mixer.
Pre-set ritual?
Words of affirmation. That's it. Mind right before the booth.
Favorite late-night food after a set?
Rally's or Portillo's. No debate.
What are you working on right now?
Releases and mixes. More shows. Hopefully going on tours and traveling. It's all a work in progress — but it's moving forward every day.
