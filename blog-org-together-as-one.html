About Together As One

Together As One is Los Angeles' iconic New Year's Eve warehouse rave, one of the longest-running New Year's events in the California underground scene. Held at large warehouse venues in the LA area, TAO brings together thousands of ravers to celebrate the New Year with music, art, and community.

Known for massive production, diverse lineups spanning multiple genres, and an inclusive rave culture ethos, Together As One has become an annual pilgrimage for California's underground community. It represents everything the California rave scene stands for.

What They Do

Annual New Year's Eve warehouse rave production, multi-stage event management, and underground rave culture preservation in Los Angeles.

Upcoming Events

Together As One takes place every New Year's Eve in Los Angeles. Follow @togetherasone for venue announcements and ticket sales each fall.

How long has Together As One been running?

Multi-genre — house, trance, techno, bass, and more across multiple stages.
