JOHN BRYARS

Mechanized Euphoria

Los Angeles, CA
Acid / Techno / Electro / EBM / Breaks
KEEPITIL Artist

John Bryars makes club music that feels mechanized. The Los Angeles DJ and producer moves between acid, techno, electro, EBM, breaks and trance, building sets that carry industrial grit and ambient weight in the same breath. Where a lot of hard music settles for force alone, Bryars chases something closer to euphoria — the moment a machine sounds like it is reaching for feeling. It has made him one of the most talked-about names in LA's underground.

Acid, techno, electro and EBM, welded together with emotional weight — Bryars treats hard club music as something that can still feel like release.
THE SOUND
A wide, eclectic palette
Resident Advisor describes Bryars as a Los Angeles artist "recognized for his broad tastes and eclectic style," navigating an acid, techno, electro, EBM, breaks and trance multiverse in pursuit of euphoric, auditory bliss. Rather than locking into a single tempo or texture, his sets slip between mechanized rhythms and haze-drenched melody — the kind of range that reads as restless rather than scattered.
Machine music with emotional weight
His productions lean into metal-edged rhythms and cathartic release. The sound is unmistakably electronic — slipping between techno, acid, EBM and industrial grit — but it carries an ambient sensibility and emotional charge that keeps it from tipping into pure aggression. That balance of physical impact and feeling is the throughline of his work.
THE RESUME
The Boysnoize breakout
Bryars' breakout moment came with two tracks alongside fellow LA club disruptor Pilo on the X EP for Boysnoize Records — a release later remixed by Schacke and Moby that quickly put him on the radar of underground selectors well beyond California.
Aeonic Forme
He now runs Aeonic Forme, his own Los Angeles label dedicated to experimentation and transformation. The imprint launched with his EP Invariant, a four-track surge of haze-drenched electricity, with his album ZONE IV also available via Bandcamp.
Stages, local and international
In LA he has played some of the city's most iconic parties — A Club Called Rhonda, Boiler Room, Mixmag Lab, Lights Down Low, Midnight Lovers and Future Primitive — plus festivals like Sunset Campout. Internationally he has appeared at Kraftwerk Berlin, Kinky Sundays at PAL in Hamburg and Funk club in Mexico City. In early 2026 he was featured on Beatportal's "Eight Emerging Artists On Our Radar" list.
THE SCENE
Part of LA's underground surge
Bryars sits squarely inside the wave of Los Angeles artists pushing harder, more confrontational electronic music through the city's warehouse and DIY circuit — a scene that has bounced back stronger than ever and is increasingly drawing international attention. KEEPITIL features him as part of documenting that moment in California's underground.
