About Rave Clothing Co

Rave Clothing Co is a Burbank-based rave and festival fashion brand creating bold, expressive clothing for California's rave community. From UV-reactive sets to handmade accessories, Rave Clothing Co serves the style-conscious underground crowd that wants to look as good as they feel.

The brand has become a fixture at Southern California events, with pop-up shops at raves and festivals throughout the year. Their online store ships nationally, making OC and LA underground fashion accessible to ravers everywhere.

What They Offer

Rave wear, festival fashion, UV-reactive clothing, handmade accessories, and underground fashion retail for California's rave and festival community.

Rave sets and festival outfits (UV-reactive)
