4 Free Ways to Support the Underground (July 2026)

KEEPITILJuly 2, 2026OC / LA / SoCal

Nobody in the underground got here for the money — but the scene still runs on it. Renting warehouses, pressing tracks, paying door staff, hauling sound systems out to the desert: all of that costs real cash. The good news is there are ways to back the culture that cost you nothing, and a few that actually put money or discounts in your pocket. Here are the four we use and stand behind right now, whether you're an artist, an organizer, or the person front-left of the sub every weekend.
