ABOUT DESERT BLOOM SUPPLY

Desert Bloom Supply is a San Bernardino-based supplier of festival and event goods serving the Inland Empire and broader SoCal underground scene — from stage materials and decor to practical supplies that event organizers need and can't always find at mainstream retailers.

Founded by an IE event organizer who spent years sourcing event supplies from multiple scattered vendors, Desert Bloom Supply consolidates what underground promoters actually need: LED lighting, staging materials, cable management, tent structures, and the hundred small items that make an outdoor or warehouse event function safely and look right.

WHAT THEY OFFER
LED lighting and atmosphere effects
Staging and structural materials
Cable management and power distribution
Tent and shelter structures
Event signage and wayfinding
Consumable event supplies

Q&A
Who's your typical customer?

Underground event organizers in the IE, OC, and SD corridor who are running events without the budget of major festival productions but who take the experience seriously. We specialize in solutions that look and perform well at a price point that works for independent promoters.

What gap in the market does Desert Bloom fill?

Professional-grade event supply that's accessible to independent promoters rather than only to corporate production companies. The big supply houses aren't interested in orders from a 300-person warehouse party. We are.
