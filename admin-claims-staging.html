Claim Approvals

Admin sign in

Sign in with your KEEPITIL admin account to review profile claims.

How approval works: Approving sets the claim to approved and grants the claimant edit access by writing the ownership link (profile_owners). The claimant must have a KEEPITIL account under the same email — if they don't yet, they'll be prompted to sign up, and access links automatically. Once linked, their profile upgrades to the new editable template. Rejecting marks the claim closed.

Note: writing ownership requires your admin session to have insert rights on profile_owners (RLS admin policy). If a grant fails with a permissions error, that policy needs to be enabled in Supabase.
